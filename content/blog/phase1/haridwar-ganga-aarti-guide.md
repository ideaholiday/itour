Haridwar, "the gateway to the gods", is where the Ganga leaves the Himalayan foothills and enters the plains. It is one of the seven holiest cities in Hinduism, one of the four Kumbh Mela sites, and the starting point for many pilgrims heading to the Char Dham. For most visitors, the heart of the city is the evening **Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri**.

## How to reach Haridwar

- **By train:** Haridwar Junction has direct trains from Delhi (including the Shatabdi and Vande Bharat), Mumbai, Kolkata and other cities.
- **By road:** Delhi is about 220 km away, 5 to 6 hours by car or bus depending on traffic.
- **By air:** Jolly Grant Airport near Dehradun is about 35 km away.

Rishikesh is only about 25 km upstream, so most travellers combine the two. See our [Rishikesh yoga and adventure guide](/blog/rishikesh-yoga-adventure-guide).

## The Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri

Har Ki Pauri ("the steps of Hari") is the most sacred ghat in Haridwar, where Vishnu's footprint is said to be set in stone. Every evening at sunset, priests wave large brass lamps over the river while bells ring and thousands of people sing. Small leaf boats filled with flowers and diyas are then set afloat on the Ganga.

**How to enjoy it:**

- Arrive at least an hour early to find a place on the ghat or on the bridge opposite.
- Keep your shoes in the free shoe stands and your valuables close; it gets very crowded.
- The aarti time changes with sunset, so check locally on the day.
- Volunteers may ask for donations. Give only to official Ganga Sabha counters if you wish to donate.

A morning dip at Har Ki Pauri is much calmer, and the chains and railings along the steps are there for a reason: the current is strong.

## Temples and places to see

### Mansa Devi Temple

On Bilwa Parvat hill, dedicated to the goddess who fulfils wishes. Devotees tie threads on the sacred tree here. You can climb the steps or take the **ropeway (udan khatola)**, which also gives a great view over the city and the river.

### Chandi Devi Temple

On Neel Parvat, on the other side of the Ganga, reached by another ropeway. Combo tickets for both ropeways are usually available.

### Maya Devi Temple

One of the Shakti Peethas and one of the oldest temples in Haridwar, close to Har Ki Pauri.

### Bharat Mata Mandir

An unusual eight-storey temple dedicated to Mother India, with floors on freedom fighters, saints and the gods of different traditions. A lift goes to the top.

### Daksha Mahadev Temple, Kankhal

In the old town of Kankhal, linked to the legend of Sati and Daksha. Kankhal's lanes also have beautiful old havelis and ashrams.

### Shantikunj and Patanjali Yogpeeth

Large ashram campuses that welcome visitors interested in yoga, meditation and Ayurveda.

## Day trips from Haridwar

- **Rishikesh (25 km):** Ram Jhula, Triveni Ghat aarti, cafés, yoga and river rafting. Read our [Rishikesh river rafting guide](/blog/rishikesh-river-rafting-guide).
- **Rajaji National Park:** jeep safaris through the Chilla range to see elephants, deer and birds. The park is usually open from mid-November to mid-June.
- **Char Dham Yatra:** Haridwar is the traditional starting point for Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath. Registration is mandatory for the yatra, so register on the official Uttarakhand portal before you go.

## Food in Haridwar

Haridwar is a vegetarian city, and alcohol and meat are not sold near the ghats. Try kachori-aloo and jalebi at the old shops of Moti Bazaar, chole-bhature, kulhad lassi, and the rabri and sweets near Har Ki Pauri.

## Best time to visit

October to April has the most pleasant weather. The Kanwar Yatra in July brings enormous crowds and traffic restrictions, so avoid it unless you are joining it. The Kumbh (every 12 years) and Ardh Kumbh (every 6 years) are the biggest events. The monsoon can cause landslides on hill roads further up.

## A one-day plan

1. Morning dip or walk at Har Ki Pauri.
2. Ropeway to Mansa Devi, then Chandi Devi.
3. Lunch in Moti Bazaar.
4. Afternoon at Bharat Mata Mandir and Kankhal.
5. Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri in the evening.

## Frequently asked questions

### What time is the Ganga Aarti in Haridwar?

It takes place at sunset, so the time changes through the year. There is also a morning aarti. Check locally and arrive early.

### Haridwar or Rishikesh: which is better for the aarti?

Haridwar's Har Ki Pauri aarti is larger and more intense. Rishikesh's aartis at Triveni Ghat and Parmarth Niketan are calmer. If you have time, see both.

### How many days do I need in Haridwar?

One day covers the main sights. Two days lets you add Rishikesh or a Rajaji safari.
