Lucknow, Ayodhya and Varanasi make one of the most rewarding short circuits in India. In five days you get Nawabi monuments and kebabs in Lucknow, the Ram Mandir and the Saryu ghats in Ayodhya, and the Ganga aarti and ancient lanes of Kashi. The route is simple to drive, works well for families and senior citizens, and can start or end at any of the three cities, all of which have airports.

## The route at a glance

- **Lucknow to Ayodhya:** about 135 km, roughly 3 hours by road.
- **Ayodhya to Varanasi:** about 200 km, roughly 4 to 5 hours by road.
- **Optional detour:** Prayagraj sits between Ayodhya and Varanasi on some routes and is about 120 km from Varanasi.

A private cab with a driver for the whole trip is the easiest option. It lets you start darshan early, carry luggage safely while you are inside temples, and rest between stops.

## Day 1: Arrive in Lucknow

Fly or take the train into Lucknow and check in. In the afternoon visit the **Bara Imambara** with its famous Bhool Bhulaiya maze, the **Rumi Darwaza** and the **Chhota Imambara**, which is beautiful when lit up in the evening. Finish at **Hazratganj** for a stroll, and then eat your way through Aminabad or Chowk: galouti kebabs, kulchas with nihari, and malai makhan in winter.

For a detailed plan of the city, read our [one-day Lucknow guide](/blog/one-day-in-lucknow).

## Day 2: Lucknow to Ayodhya

Leave early after breakfast and reach Ayodhya by late morning. Start with **Hanuman Garhi**, where tradition says you should pay respects before the Ram Mandir, and then go for **Ram Mandir darshan**. Phones, bags and electronics are not allowed inside, so leave them in the cab or the cloakroom.

After lunch, visit **Kanak Bhawan** and **Dashrath Mahal**. In the evening, attend the **Saryu aarti** and walk along **Ram ki Paidi**, which is lit up beautifully at night. Stay the night in Ayodhya.

Our [Ayodhya Ram Mandir darshan guide](/blog/ayodhya-ram-mandir-darshan-guide) has more detail on timings, crowds and what to carry.

## Day 3: Ayodhya to Varanasi

Take a sunrise boat ride on the Saryu or visit Guptar Ghat before leaving. Drive to Varanasi and check in near the ghats if you can: staying within walking distance of Dashashwamedh or Assi Ghat makes early mornings much easier.

In the evening, watch the **Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat**. Arrive at least 45 minutes early for a good spot on the steps, or book a boat to watch from the river.

## Day 4: Varanasi

- **Sunrise boat ride** along the ghats, from Assi to Manikarnika.
- **Kashi Vishwanath Temple** darshan through the Kashi Vishwanath Dham corridor. Go early and travel light: phones and bags are not allowed.
- **Walk the old lanes** around Vishwanath Gali for banarasi silk, brassware and street food: kachori-sabzi, tamatar chaat, malaiyyo in winter and a Banarasi paan.
- **Afternoon in Sarnath**, about 10 km away, where the Buddha gave his first sermon. See the Dhamekh Stupa and the Sarnath Museum with the Lion Capital of Ashoka.
- **Evening:** a quieter aarti at Assi Ghat, or a second look at Dashashwamedh.

Our [Varanasi spiritual guide](/blog/varanasi-spiritual-guide) covers the ghats and temples in depth.

## Day 5: Depart

Fit in a last morning walk on the ghats before flying or taking the train home from Varanasi.

## Adding Prayagraj: a 6-day version

With one extra day, go from Ayodhya to **Prayagraj**, take a boat to the **Triveni Sangam**, visit the Bade Hanuman Temple and Anand Bhawan, and then continue to Varanasi the next morning. See our [Prayagraj and Triveni Sangam guide](/blog/prayagraj-triveni-sangam-guide).

## Best time for this trip

October to March is ideal. Winter mornings in December and January can be foggy, which sometimes delays trains and flights, so keep some buffer. Diwali (Deepotsav in Ayodhya and Dev Deepawali in Varanasi a fortnight later) is magical but very crowded. Summers are very hot.

## Tips for a smooth trip

- Book stays early around Ram Navami, Diwali, Dev Deepawali and long weekends.
- Plan temple visits for early morning, when queues are shortest.
- Keep modest clothing and slip-on footwear handy.
- Carry some cash for small offerings, boats and street food.
- For senior citizens, ask about wheelchairs and assistance at the major temples.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is 5 days enough for Lucknow, Ayodhya and Varanasi?

Yes. Five days covers the main sights at a comfortable pace. Add a sixth day for Prayagraj.

### Should I start in Lucknow or Varanasi?

Either works. Starting in Lucknow and ending in Varanasi saves the most memorable city, and the Ganga aarti, for last.

### Can this trip be done by train?

Yes. All three cities are linked by rail, but a cab gives you more flexibility for early darshan and luggage.
