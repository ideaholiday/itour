Prayagraj, long known as Allahabad, sits where the Ganga and the Yamuna meet the mythical Saraswati at the **Triveni Sangam**. Hindus consider a dip here especially sacred, and the city hosts the Kumbh Mela every twelve years and the Magh Mela every winter. Away from the river, Prayagraj is also a city of Mughal forts, colonial buildings, a famous university and key sites from India's freedom struggle.

## How to reach Prayagraj

- **By train:** Prayagraj Junction is a major stop on the Delhi–Howrah line, with direct trains from Delhi, Kolkata, Mumbai, Lucknow and Varanasi.
- **By air:** Prayagraj Airport (Bamrauli) has flights to several metros. Varanasi and Lucknow airports have more options.
- **By road:** Varanasi is about 120 km away and Lucknow about 200 km.

## The Triveni Sangam

The confluence is easy to see from a boat: the Ganga is muddier and the Yamuna greener, and you can watch the two meet. Boats leave from the ghats near Allahabad Fort, and boatmen take you out to wooden platforms in the middle of the river where pilgrims take a holy dip and perform rituals.

**Tips for the Sangam:**

- Agree the boat fare and the length of the ride before you get in. Fares are per boat or per person, and a shared boat is cheaper.
- Early morning is calmer and more beautiful, especially in winter when migratory Siberian gulls gather over the water.
- Carry a change of clothes and a small towel if you plan to take a dip.
- Offer and buy flowers and diyas at the ghat rather than on the boat.

## Kumbh Mela and Magh Mela

The **Kumbh Mela** in Prayagraj takes place every 12 years, with an Ardh Kumbh at the six-year mark. The most recent Maha Kumbh was held from January to February 2025 and drew hundreds of millions of pilgrims.

Every year in the Hindu month of Magh (usually January to February), the **Magh Mela** turns the Sangam's sandbanks into a tent city. Kalpvasis live there for the month, and major bathing days such as Makar Sankranti, Mauni Amavasya and Basant Panchami draw huge crowds. It is a smaller, more manageable way to see the atmosphere of the Kumbh.

## Things to do in Prayagraj

### Allahabad Fort, Patalpuri Temple and Akshayavat

Built by Akbar in the 16th century on the banks of the Yamuna, the fort is mostly used by the Army, but pilgrims can visit the underground **Patalpuri Temple** and the sacred **Akshayavat**, the "indestructible banyan tree". Check access rules before you go, as entry is controlled.

### Bade Hanuman Temple

Near the Sangam, this temple has a large reclining idol of Hanuman. It is said the Ganga rises to touch the idol during the monsoon floods. Visit it together with the Sangam.

### Anand Bhawan and Swaraj Bhawan

The Nehru family's ancestral homes are now museums about the freedom struggle, with original furniture, letters and photographs. The adjoining Jawahar Planetarium is a hit with children.

### Chandrashekhar Azad Park and Allahabad Museum

The large park, formerly Alfred Park, is where revolutionary Chandrashekhar Azad died in 1931. The Allahabad Museum inside it has good sculpture and freedom-movement galleries.

### Khusro Bagh

A walled Mughal garden with the sandstone tombs of Prince Khusrau, his mother Shah Begum and his sister. It is quiet, beautiful and rarely crowded.

### Evening at the ghats

Watch the evening aarti at the Sangam area or at Ram Ghat, and finish with Prayagraj's street food at Civil Lines or Loknath: kachori, chaat, dahi jalebi and the city's famous guavas in winter.

## Suggested one-day plan

1. Sunrise boat to the Triveni Sangam.
2. Bade Hanuman Temple and Patalpuri Temple.
3. Anand Bhawan and Swaraj Bhawan after breakfast.
4. Khusro Bagh and Chandrashekhar Azad Park in the afternoon.
5. Evening aarti and street food at Civil Lines.

## Best time to visit

October to March is the most pleasant, and January to February brings the Magh Mela. The monsoon (July to September) brings high water levels, which can limit boat trips. April to June is very hot.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much time do I need in Prayagraj?

One full day covers the Sangam and the main sights. Stay two days during the Magh Mela.

### Is it safe to bathe at the Sangam?

Pilgrims bathe at the Sangam every day, using the platforms and marked bathing areas. Stay within them, follow the boatman's advice and avoid going out when the water is high.

### What is the difference between Kumbh and Magh Mela?

The Kumbh is held every 12 years and is far larger. The Magh Mela takes place every year at the same spot in January and February.
