White-water rafting on the Ganga is the most popular adventure activity in Rishikesh, and for good reason. Within a couple of hours of Delhi by air, or a long drive, you can be paddling through rapids with names like Roller Coaster and Golf Course, surrounded by forested Himalayan foothills. This guide explains how to choose the right stretch, when to go and how to do it safely.

## The rafting stretches

All trips end near Rishikesh town; the difference is where you start and how many rapids you run. Distances are approximate:

- **Brahmpuri to Rishikesh (about 9 km):** the shortest trip, with gentle Grade I and II rapids. Good for families and nervous first-timers.
- **Shivpuri to Rishikesh (about 16 km):** the most popular stretch. It includes the big-name rapids of **Roller Coaster** and **Golf Course**, plus a chance for body surfing and cliff jumping on calm sections. Takes about two to three hours on the water.
- **Marine Drive to Rishikesh (about 24 km):** adds more rapids and more time on the river. Good if you want a half day.
- **Kaudiyala to Rishikesh (about 35 km):** the longest day trip, with the toughest rapids on this part of the Ganga, including **The Wall** and **Three Blind Mice**. For fit, confident swimmers who have rafted before.

Multi-day rafting and camping trips are also available from further upstream.

## Famous rapids to look out for

- **Roller Coaster (Grade III):** a series of big waves, the highlight of the Shivpuri stretch.
- **Golf Course (Grade III to III+):** a long, bumpy rapid right after Roller Coaster.
- **Club House and Return to Sender:** fun rapids on the Shivpuri stretch.
- **The Wall and Three Blind Mice:** on the Kaudiyala stretch, powerful rapids for experienced rafters.

## The rafting season

Rafting in Rishikesh usually runs from **around mid-September to June**. It is suspended during the monsoon, typically from July to mid-September, when the Ganga is high and fast; the exact dates are set by the state authorities each year.

- **October to November:** clear water and pleasant weather; a great time.
- **December to February:** cold water and chilly mornings, but quiet and scenic. Wetsuits help.
- **March to June:** warm weather and the most popular period, with more water in the river and livelier rapids later in the season.

## Age, health and swimming

- Operators set minimum ages by stretch; families with children should choose the shorter Brahmpuri stretch and confirm the age limit with the operator.
- You do not need to be a swimmer, because life jackets are mandatory, but you should be comfortable in water and able to follow instructions.
- Tell the guide about any heart condition, back problem, pregnancy or recent surgery. Rafting is not suitable in these cases.

## Choosing a safe rafting operator

Safety standards vary, so choose carefully:

- Use operators **licensed by the Uttarakhand tourism authorities**, with trained, certified river guides.
- Check that the group has a **safety kayaker** on bigger stretches.
- Check your equipment: a properly fitted life jacket, a helmet and a good paddle.
- Listen to the safety briefing and follow your guide's commands, especially "hold on" and "get down".
- Avoid alcohol before rafting.

## What to wear and carry

- Quick-dry clothes (avoid cotton and jeans), and a change of clothes for afterwards.
- Sports sandals or floaters with straps; flip-flops will come off.
- Sunscreen and sunglasses with a strap.
- A waterproof pouch for your phone, or leave it in the vehicle.
- A light jacket in winter.

## Rafting and camping

Many operators run **riverside camps** in the Shivpuri area, combining rafting with bonfires, beach volleyball and simple meals. A night or two at a camp is a fun way to add rafting to a weekend trip.

## Other adventures in Rishikesh

- **Bungee jumping** and the giant swing at Mohan Chatti.
- **Cliff jumping** on the rafting route.
- **Trekking** to Neer Garh waterfall or Kunjapuri temple at sunrise.
- **Zip-lining** and **camping** in the hills.

## Getting to Rishikesh

Rishikesh is about 240 km from Delhi by road. Jolly Grant Airport near Dehradun is about 20 km away, and Haridwar, the nearest major railway station, is about 25 km. Read our [Haridwar guide](/blog/haridwar-ganga-aarti-guide) if you want to add the Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which rafting stretch is best for beginners?

Shivpuri to Rishikesh is the best balance of fun and safety for most first-timers. Families with young children should choose Brahmpuri to Rishikesh.

### Is river rafting in Rishikesh safe?

Yes, when you use a licensed operator, wear a helmet and life jacket, and follow your guide.

### Can I raft in Rishikesh in July or August?

Usually not. Rafting is suspended during the monsoon, generally from July to mid-September.

### Do I need to know how to swim?

No, you will be wearing a life jacket. But you should be comfortable in water and follow instructions.
