**Kaziranga National Park** in Assam is one of the great wildlife destinations in Asia and a **UNESCO World Heritage Site** since 1985. Its tall elephant grass, marshes and forests along the Brahmaputra are home to about **two-thirds of the world's greater one-horned rhinos**, along with wild elephants, wild water buffalo, swamp deer and one of the highest densities of tigers anywhere. Unlike many parks, you are almost certain to see rhinos here.

## How to reach Kaziranga

- **By air:** **Jorhat** Airport is about 95 km from Kohora, the park's central gateway. **Guwahati** has more flights and is about 200 km away, roughly 5 hours by road.
- **By train:** Furkating and Jakhalabandha are the nearest stations. Guwahati has the best connections.
- **By road:** the national highway runs along the southern edge of the park, and you often see rhinos and deer from the road.

Most stays are around **Kohora** (central range) and **Bagori** (western range).

## The safari ranges

Kaziranga is divided into ranges, each with its own gate and character:

- **Central Range (Kohora):** the most popular, with a good mix of grassland, wetlands and forest; excellent for rhinos, elephants and buffalo.
- **Western Range (Bagori):** open grasslands with the highest density of rhinos; great for photography.
- **Eastern Range (Agaratoli):** wetlands full of birds, including pelicans and storks; good for birdwatchers.
- **Burapahar Range:** hilly forest in the west, quieter and good for primates and birds.

Plan at least two to three safaris in different ranges.

## Safari options

- **Jeep safaris:** the main way to explore the park, with morning and afternoon slots. Book through your lodge, licensed operators or the official booking channels.
- **Elephant safaris:** early-morning rides take visitors into the tall grass close to rhinos. Some travellers choose not to ride elephants for welfare reasons; jeep safaris are an excellent alternative.
- **Birdwatching walks:** in the areas around the park, with local guides.

## When is Kaziranga open?

The park is usually open from **November 1 to April 30** and closes during the monsoon, when the Brahmaputra floods much of it. Some ranges may open in October, depending on conditions.

- **November to February:** cool, pleasant weather and migratory birds. Mornings can be foggy.
- **March and April:** hotter, but the grass is burnt back, making animals easier to see.

## What you can see

- **Greater one-horned rhinoceros**, everywhere in the grasslands.
- **Wild Asian elephants**, often in large herds.
- **Wild water buffalo** and **eastern swamp deer (barasingha)**.
- **Tigers**, though they are shy and hard to see in the tall grass.
- **Hoolock gibbons** in the nearby Hollongapar Gibbon Sanctuary.
- **Birds:** bar-headed geese, pelicans, adjutant storks, hornbills and many raptors.

## Tips for a good safari

- **Book early** for December and January.
- **Carry binoculars**, a zoom lens, a hat and layers for chilly mornings.
- **Wear muted colours** and stay quiet.
- **Stay in the jeep** and follow the guide's and forest guard's instructions.
- **Never feed animals** or throw litter.

## Around Kaziranga

- **Kaziranga Orchid and Biodiversity Park:** hundreds of orchid species, plus Assamese food and culture shows.
- **Tea gardens:** stay at or visit a tea estate around Kaziranga or Jorhat.
- **Majuli:** one of the world's largest river islands, famous for its Vaishnavite satras, mask-making and river life, reached by ferry from near Jorhat.
- **Hollongapar Gibbon Sanctuary:** home to India's only ape, the hoolock gibbon.

## A 3-day plan

1. **Guwahati to Kaziranga**; afternoon jeep safari in the Central Range.
2. **Early elephant or jeep safari** in the Western Range; afternoon in the Eastern Range for birds; orchid park in the evening.
3. **Morning safari in Burapahar** or a tea garden visit, then drive back to Guwahati or on to Majuli.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is Kaziranga National Park open?

Usually from November 1 to April 30. It is closed during the monsoon floods.

### Which is the best range in Kaziranga?

The Central Range (Kohora) and Western Range (Bagori) are best for rhinos and elephants. The Eastern Range is best for birds.

### How many days do I need in Kaziranga?

Two to three days allows three or four safaris across different ranges.
