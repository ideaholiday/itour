Gokarna, on the Karnataka coast south of Goa, is two places in one. The old town is a sacred pilgrimage centre built around the **Mahabaleshwar Temple**, with a Shiva lingam believed to be the Atmalinga. Just beyond it, a chain of quiet crescent beaches, linked by cliff-top trails, draws travellers looking for a calmer alternative to Goa.

## How to reach Gokarna

- **By train:** **Gokarna Road** station on the Konkan Railway is about 10 km from town, with trains from Mumbai, Goa, Mangaluru and Bengaluru. Kumta and Ankola are other nearby stations.
- **By air:** Goa's airports are about 3 to 4 hours away by road. Hubballi is another option.
- **By road:** overnight buses run from Bengaluru, Mumbai and Goa.

## The temples of Gokarna

- **Mahabaleshwar Temple:** one of the most sacred Shiva temples in South India, holding the **Atmalinga** that, according to legend, Ravana was tricked into setting down here. Men are usually asked to remove their shirts before entering the inner shrine, and traditional dress is expected.
- **Maha Ganapati Temple:** next door, traditionally visited before Mahabaleshwar.
- **Koti Tirtha:** the temple tank where pilgrims bathe.
- **Gokarna Beach (the main beach):** the town beach where pilgrims take a dip before visiting the temple.

**Shivaratri** (February or March) is the biggest festival here, with a huge chariot procession.

## The beaches

South of town, the beaches get quieter one after another:

- **Kudle Beach:** the closest and most lively, with cafés, guesthouses and a long stretch of sand. Great for sunsets.
- **Om Beach:** shaped like the Om symbol when seen from above, with a mix of cafés, stays and water sports.
- **Half Moon Beach:** a small, secluded beach reached by trail or boat, with a few simple shacks.
- **Paradise Beach:** a rocky, remote beach, reached on foot or by boat; very basic facilities.

## The Gokarna beach trek

The famous **beach trek** links the beaches along cliff-top trails: **Gokarna Beach → Kudle → Om → Half Moon → Paradise**. The full walk is about **8 to 10 km** and takes most people 4 to 6 hours with stops.

**Tips:**

- Start early to avoid the midday heat.
- Wear good walking sandals or shoes; some sections are rocky and slippery.
- Carry water, sunscreen and a hat.
- Take a boat back from Paradise or Half Moon to Om Beach, or return along the trail before dark.
- Don't walk the trail after dark.

## Sea safety

The sea around Gokarna can be **rough, with strong currents and rip tides**, and drownings have happened at these beaches. Swim only where lifeguards are present and it is safe, never swim after drinking, and stay out of the water in the monsoon. Watch out for slippery rocks at high tide.

## Things to do

- **Sunset at Kudle or Om Beach.**
- **Kayaking and boat rides** from Om Beach, with dolphin sightings on some trips.
- **Yoga classes** at retreats around Kudle and Om.
- **Mirjan Fort:** a moss-covered 16th-century fort about 25 km away.
- **Yana Rocks:** dramatic black karst rock formations in the forest, about 50 km away, reached by a short walk.
- **Vibhooti Falls** near Yana, in and after the monsoon.

## Where to stay

- **Kudle and Om beaches:** beach huts, cottages and a few upmarket resorts.
- **Gokarna town:** guesthouses, closer to the temples and the bus stand.
- **Half Moon and Paradise:** very basic huts, if you want a truly remote night; facilities are limited and rules on stays here change, so check first.

## What to eat

The town is mostly vegetarian, with **thali meals** at simple restaurants. The beach cafés serve seafood, Israeli food, pancakes and smoothies. Try **Udupi-style breakfasts** and **fish thalis** in the nearby towns.

## Best time to visit

**October to March** is the best time: calm seas and pleasant weather. The monsoon (June to September) brings very heavy rain and rough seas; many beach shacks close, and swimming is dangerous. Summers (April and May) are hot and humid.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many days are enough for Gokarna?

Two to three days: one for the temples and town, one for the beach trek, and one to relax.

### Is it safe to swim at Om Beach?

Only in calm conditions, in areas where lifeguards are present. Currents can be strong, especially in the monsoon.

### Is Gokarna better than Goa?

Gokarna is quieter, smaller and more spiritual. Goa has more nightlife, water sports and variety. Many travellers visit both.
