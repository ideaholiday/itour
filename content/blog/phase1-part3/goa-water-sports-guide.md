Goa is India's favourite place to try water sports. Its busy north-coast beaches have everything from parasailing and jet skis to banana boats, while boat trips head out to **Grande Island** for snorkelling and try dives, and quieter operators run kayaking tours through mangroves and backwaters. This guide explains what to try, where to go and how to stay safe.

For everything else in Goa, from spice farms to churches, see our [Goa beyond the beach guide](/blog/goa-beyond-beach-guide).

## Popular water sports

### Parasailing

You are harnessed to a parachute and towed by a speedboat, rising high above the sea for a few minutes. Most parasailing in Goa is launched from a boat, so you take off and land on the deck. **Calangute, Baga and Candolim** are the main beaches.

### Jet skiing

Ride a jet ski with an instructor or, in some places, on your own in a marked zone. Rides are short, usually a few minutes.

### Banana boat and bumper rides

A group rides an inflatable banana or "bumper" towed behind a speedboat, which then tips everyone into the sea. Fun for groups and families, with life jackets on.

### Speedboat rides

Short, fast rides along the coast.

### Scuba diving and snorkelling

- **Grande Island**, off the Mormugao peninsula, is Goa's main dive and snorkel spot, with shallow reefs, small wrecks and reef fish.
- **Try dives** (Discover Scuba) are available for beginners with no experience; certified divers can do fun dives.
- **Snorkelling trips** usually include a boat ride, snorkelling stops and lunch.

Visibility in Goa is lower than in the Andamans, so set your expectations accordingly. For India's best diving, see our [Andaman scuba diving guide](/blog/scuba-diving-andaman-havelock-guide).

### Dolphin trips

Boat trips look for **Indo-Pacific humpback dolphins** along the coast, from **Sinquerim, Coco Beach, Palolem** and other spots. Morning trips give the best chances. Ask boatmen not to chase or crowd the dolphins.

### Kayaking and stand-up paddle boarding

Guided kayak and SUP tours through the **mangroves and backwaters** around Nerul, Chapora and the south Goa rivers are calm and scenic, and often include birdwatching. **Palolem** and **Agonda** are good for gentle sea kayaking.

### Surfing

Goa has small, friendly waves, and a few surf schools around **Arambol, Ashwem and Morjim** offer beginner lessons, especially in the months around the monsoon.

### White-water rafting

In the monsoon, rafting runs on the **Mhadei river** in the Western Ghats, near the Goa–Karnataka border. It is seasonal, usually from about July to September, depending on water levels.

## Where to go

- **North Goa:** Calangute, Baga, Candolim and Anjuna for all the classic water sports.
- **Sinquerim and Coco Beach:** dolphin trips and boat rides.
- **Grande Island (from Bogmalo, Dona Paula or Sinquerim):** scuba and snorkelling.
- **South Goa:** Palolem, Agonda and Colva for kayaking, dolphin trips and quieter sports.

## The water sports season

Sea water sports usually run from about **October to May**. They are **suspended during the monsoon**, roughly **June to September**, when the sea is rough and dangerous. The best months are **November to March**, with calm seas and clear skies.

## Safety tips

- **Choose operators registered with Goa Tourism**, with proper licences and trained staff.
- **Always wear a life jacket**, and check that it fits and is fastened.
- **Check the equipment**: harnesses for parasailing, working kill switches on jet skis.
- **Listen to the briefing** and follow the instructions.
- **Don't do water sports after drinking alcohol.**
- **Tell the operator about health issues**, such as heart or back problems, or pregnancy.
- **Agree on the price and duration before starting**, and get a receipt.
- **Swim only in flagged areas** with lifeguards present. Goa's beaches have strong currents in places.

## Packages and pricing

Many beach operators sell **combo packages** (for example, parasailing, jet ski and banana ride together). Compare what is included and the duration of each activity, and be wary of touts on the beach. Booking through a trusted operator in advance can save time in peak season.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the best time for water sports in Goa?

November to March, when the sea is calm. Water sports are suspended in the monsoon, roughly June to September.

### Which beach is best for water sports in Goa?

Calangute and Baga in North Goa have the widest choice. Grande Island is best for scuba and snorkelling.

### Is parasailing in Goa safe?

It is generally safe with a licensed operator that uses proper equipment and follows safety rules. Avoid operators who cut corners on briefings or gear.

### Can I scuba dive in Goa without experience?

Yes. Try dives with an instructor are available for beginners at Grande Island.
