Saurashtra, the western peninsula of Gujarat, packs three of India's great travel experiences into one road trip: **Gir National Park**, the only place in the world where Asiatic lions live in the wild; **Somnath**, the first of the twelve Jyotirlingas, standing on the shore of the Arabian Sea; and **Dwarka**, the ancient kingdom of Lord Krishna and one of the four Char Dham sites.

## How to reach Saurashtra

- **By air:** Rajkot, Porbandar and Diu airports are the closest to this route. Ahmedabad has the most flights.
- **By train:** Veraval (near Somnath), Junagadh, Porbandar and Dwarka stations all have direct trains.
- **By road:** Sasan Gir is about 45 km from Somnath, and Somnath to Dwarka is about 230 km along the coast via Porbandar.

## Gir National Park

Gir is the last refuge of the **Asiatic lion**, which had been reduced to a handful of animals in the early 20th century. Thanks to protection, the population has grown and lions now range well beyond the park. Gir is also home to leopards, sambar, chital, nilgai, crocodiles and over 300 species of birds.

### Safaris

- **Gir jungle safari:** jeep safaris into the national park on fixed routes, booked on the **official Gir permit booking website**. Permits are limited and sell out fast in peak season, so book as soon as your dates are fixed. Carry the ID used for booking.
- **Devalia Safari Park (Gir Interpretation Zone):** a fenced area near Sasan with bus and jeep safaris, where you are very likely to see lions. It is a good backup if you can't get a jungle permit.
- **Ambardi Safari Park:** another interpretation zone near Dhari.

**Season:** the main park is closed during the monsoon, usually from **mid-June to mid-October**. The best time is **December to April**, and the hot months of **March to May** often give the best sightings as animals come to water.

Stay in **Sasan Gir**, where there are resorts and homestays close to the gate. Early morning safaris are the best.

## Somnath

The **Somnath Temple** is the first of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Shiva. It has been destroyed and rebuilt many times in its history; the present temple, in the Chalukya style, was completed after Independence. It stands dramatically on the seashore, and an inscription on the sea wall notes that there is no land in a straight line between here and Antarctica.

- **Aarti:** attend the morning or evening aarti.
- **Sound and light show:** held in the evening, telling the temple's history.
- **Triveni Sangam:** the confluence of three rivers near the temple.
- **Bhalka Tirth:** where Lord Krishna is believed to have been struck by an arrow before leaving his earthly body.

Phones and cameras are not allowed inside the temple; use the lockers.

**Diu**, a former Portuguese enclave with beaches and a sea fort, is about 90 km from Somnath and makes a good add-on.

## Dwarka

**Dwarka** is associated with Lord Krishna's kingdom and is one of the Char Dham and one of the seven sacred cities.

- **Dwarkadhish Temple (Jagat Mandir):** a five-storey temple on the Gomti river. The flag on its spire is changed several times a day, a ritual that devotees sponsor.
- **Gomti Ghat:** for a holy dip and the evening aarti.
- **Bet Dwarka:** an island believed to be Krishna's residence, now reached by the **Sudarshan Setu** bridge. Visit the temple and the beaches.
- **Nageshwar Jyotirlinga:** another of the twelve Jyotirlingas, with a large Shiva statue, about 17 km from Dwarka.
- **Rukmini Devi Temple:** a beautifully carved temple dedicated to Krishna's queen.
- **Shivrajpur Beach:** a Blue Flag–certified beach near Dwarka.

On the way from Somnath, stop at **Porbandar** to see **Kirti Mandir**, the house where Mahatma Gandhi was born.

## A 5-day plan

1. **Arrive at Sasan Gir** via Rajkot or Diu; evening at the resort.
2. **Morning Gir safari**, then drive to Somnath for the evening aarti and light show.
3. **Somnath morning darshan**, then drive along the coast to Dwarka, stopping at Porbandar.
4. **Dwarka:** Dwarkadhish Temple, Nageshwar, Bet Dwarka and Rukmini Temple; Gomti aarti in the evening.
5. **Shivrajpur Beach** in the morning, then depart from Jamnagar, Porbandar or Rajkot.

Add Junagadh (Uparkot Fort and the Girnar ropeway) if you have an extra day.

## What to eat

Try **Kathiyawadi food**: bajra rotla, ringan no olo (smoked aubergine), sev tameta, kadhi, and chaas (buttermilk). Food in Dwarka and Somnath is mostly vegetarian.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is Gir National Park closed?

Usually from mid-June to mid-October for the monsoon. The Devalia interpretation zone may remain open for part of that time.

### Are lion sightings guaranteed in Gir?

No sighting is guaranteed in the jungle safari, though chances are good. Devalia Safari Park gives near-certain sightings.

### How far is Dwarka from Somnath?

About 230 km, roughly 5 hours by road along the coast.
