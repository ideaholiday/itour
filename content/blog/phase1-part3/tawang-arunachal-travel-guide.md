**Tawang**, at about 3,000 metres in western Arunachal Pradesh, is one of the most spectacular destinations in the Himalaya. The journey there crosses high passes and deep valleys, and the town itself is crowned by the **Tawang Monastery**, the largest in India. Along the way are Monpa villages, frozen lakes, waterfalls and memorials to the soldiers of 1962.

## Permits you need

- **Indian citizens** need an **Inner Line Permit (ILP)** to enter Arunachal Pradesh. Apply on the **official Arunachal Pradesh e-ILP portal** before you travel, with a photo and ID. Carry printed copies.
- **Foreign nationals** need a **Protected Area Permit (PAP)**, usually arranged through a registered tour operator.
- **Bum La Pass** needs an additional permit, arranged in Tawang through the district administration and the Army. Rules and availability change, so ask your hotel or driver on arrival.

## How to reach Tawang

The usual route starts in **Guwahati** and takes two days by road, about 450 to 500 km:

**Guwahati → Tezpur → Bhalukpong (ILP check) → Bomdila or Dirang → Sela Pass → Jaswant Garh → Tawang**

- **Bhalukpong** is the entry point into Arunachal, where permits are checked.
- **Dirang** and **Bomdila** are the usual overnight stops.
- **Sela Pass** (about 4,170 m) is often snowbound in winter. The **Sela Tunnel** now makes the route more reliable year-round, but you can still drive up to the pass for the views and the frozen **Sela Lake**.

Tezpur has a small airport, and helicopter services connect Guwahati and Tawang, though they are weather-dependent.

## Places to see in Tawang

### Tawang Monastery

Founded in the 17th century, **Tawang Monastery** (Galden Namgey Lhatse) is the largest monastery in India, home to hundreds of monks. It has a huge gilded statue of the Buddha, an important library of old manuscripts, and a museum. Morning prayers are a memorable experience.

### Tawang War Memorial

A stupa-shaped memorial to the Indian soldiers who died in the **1962 war**, with a museum and an evening light and sound show.

### Urgelling Monastery

The birthplace of the **6th Dalai Lama**, a small, peaceful monastery a short drive from town.

### Madhuri Lake (Sangetsar Lake)

A beautiful high-altitude lake about 40 km from Tawang, named after the Bollywood actress after a film was shot here. It is often visited with Bum La Pass. The road is rough and snowbound at times.

### Bum La Pass

At about 4,600 m on the India–China border, **Bum La** is a historic pass. Visits need a special permit and depend on weather and security conditions.

### Nuranang (Jang) Falls

A powerful waterfall near Jang, on the way from Sela Pass.

### Jaswant Garh

A memorial to **Rifleman Jaswant Singh Rawat**, who held off enemy forces during the 1962 war. The Army serves tea to travellers here.

## On the way: Dirang and Bomdila

- **Dirang:** a valley town with an old stone village (Dirang Dzong), hot springs, apple orchards, and a yak research centre.
- **Bomdila:** a hill town with monasteries and views of the Himalaya.

## Best time to visit

- **March to June:** pleasant weather and blooming rhododendrons.
- **September to October:** clear skies and the best mountain views.
- **November to February:** snow at the passes and very cold nights; beautiful, but routes can close.
- **Monsoon (July to August):** landslides are common on the roads.

The **Tawang Festival**, usually in October or November, showcases Monpa culture, and **Losar** (Tibetan New Year, February or March) is celebrated at the monastery.

## A 6-day plan

1. **Guwahati to Dirang** (via Tezpur and Bhalukpong).
2. **Dirang to Tawang** via Sela Pass, Jaswant Garh and Jang Falls.
3. **Tawang:** monastery, War Memorial and Urgelling.
4. **Madhuri Lake and Bum La** (with permits).
5. **Tawang to Dirang or Bomdila.**
6. **Back to Guwahati.**

## Health and safety

- **Acclimatise** at Dirang before crossing Sela Pass, and watch for altitude sickness.
- **Carry warm clothes**, even in summer.
- **Drive in daylight**, and follow local advice on road conditions.
- **Carry cash**; ATMs are limited outside Tawang and Bomdila.
- **Respect monastery rules**: dress modestly and ask before photographing monks.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do Indians need a permit for Tawang?

Yes. Indian citizens need an Inner Line Permit, which can be applied for online on the official Arunachal Pradesh portal.

### How many days are needed for Tawang?

About six days from Guwahati, including two days each way on the road.

### Is Sela Pass open in winter?

The pass often has snow in winter, but the Sela Tunnel keeps the route to Tawang open more reliably. Check conditions before you go.
