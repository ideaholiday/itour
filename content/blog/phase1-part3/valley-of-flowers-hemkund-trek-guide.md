The **Valley of Flowers** in Uttarakhand is a high Himalayan valley that bursts into colour every monsoon, with hundreds of species of wildflowers carpeting its meadows. Together with the neighbouring Nanda Devi National Park, it is a **UNESCO World Heritage Site**. Close by, at about 4,160 metres, the Sikh shrine of **Hemkund Sahib** stands beside a glacial lake. The two are usually combined into one moderate trek of four to six days.

## When to go

- **The valley is open from about June 1 to October 31** each year.
- **Mid-July to mid-August** is the peak flowering season, with the most variety and colour.
- **June:** fewer flowers, but snow patches and waterfalls; the trail can be muddy.
- **September:** flowers fade but skies are clearer, with autumn colours.

The trek falls in the monsoon, so expect rain, and check for landslide alerts on the road from Rishikesh.

**Hemkund Sahib** is usually open from around late May to early October; exact dates are announced each year.

## How to get there

1. **Rishikesh or Haridwar to Joshimath or Govindghat:** a long day's drive of about 10 hours on mountain roads. Many trekkers break the journey at Srinagar (Garhwal) or Rudraprayag.
2. **Govindghat to Pulna:** a short drive to the trailhead.
3. **Pulna to Ghangaria:** a trek of about 10 km on a stone path along the Lakshman Ganga, with a climb of about 1,000 metres. Ponies and porters are available, and there is a **helicopter service** from Govindghat to Ghangaria, weather permitting.

**Ghangaria** is the base village for both the Valley of Flowers and Hemkund Sahib, with guesthouses, GMVN accommodation and gurudwara lodging.

## The Valley of Flowers trek

- **From Ghangaria**, the valley entrance is a short walk away, and the trail climbs gently into the valley for about **4 to 5 km**.
- **Entry permit:** pay the fee at the forest checkpost at the start of the trail. Entry is only allowed during the day, and you must return to Ghangaria the same evening. **Camping and overnight stays inside the valley are not allowed.**
- **What you will see:** blue poppies, cobra lilies, primulas, geraniums, marigolds, balsams and many more, as well as waterfalls, glaciers and, if you are lucky, Himalayan birds and butterflies.
- **Pushpawati river** runs through the valley. Walk as far as time allows, but leave enough time to come back before the gate closes.
- The grave of **Joan Margaret Legge**, a British botanist who died here in 1939, lies in the valley.

## The Hemkund Sahib trek

- **From Ghangaria to Hemkund Sahib** is about **6 km** with a steep climb of over 1,100 metres, taking most people 4 to 6 hours up.
- **Hemkund Sahib** is an important Sikh shrine associated with Guru Gobind Singh, beside the glacial **Hemkund lake**, surrounded by seven peaks. There is also a small **Lakshman temple**.
- **Start early** and come down by early afternoon, as the weather changes quickly. Overnight stays at Hemkund are not allowed.
- The gurudwara serves hot tea and khichdi langar to pilgrims.

## Difficulty and fitness

The trek is rated **easy to moderate**. The paths are well defined, but the climbs to Ghangaria and especially to Hemkund are steep and at high altitude. Prepare with a few weeks of walking, stair climbing or cardio before you go.

## A typical 6-day plan from Rishikesh

1. **Rishikesh to Joshimath or Govindghat.**
2. **Govindghat to Ghangaria** (drive to Pulna, trek about 10 km).
3. **Valley of Flowers** day trek; back to Ghangaria.
4. **Hemkund Sahib** trek; back to Ghangaria.
5. **Ghangaria to Govindghat**, and drive to Joshimath or on to **Badrinath** (about 25 km from Govindghat).
6. **Drive back to Rishikesh.**

## What to pack

- A good waterproof jacket, rain pants and a poncho.
- Waterproof trekking shoes with grip.
- Warm layers for the mornings and for Hemkund.
- A rain cover for your backpack and dry bags for electronics.
- A walking stick, water bottle, snacks and basic medicines, including for altitude.
- Cash; ATMs are limited beyond Joshimath.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the best time to visit the Valley of Flowers?

Mid-July to mid-August, when the most flowers are in bloom.

### Can I stay overnight in the Valley of Flowers?

No. Visitors must return to Ghangaria the same day; camping inside the valley is not allowed.

### Is the Valley of Flowers trek difficult?

It is easy to moderate. The climb to Hemkund Sahib is the hardest part.

### How many days do I need?

Five to six days from Rishikesh for both the Valley of Flowers and Hemkund Sahib.
