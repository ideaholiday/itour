Mahabaleshwar, at about 1,350 metres in the Sahyadri hills, is Maharashtra's best-known hill station. It is famous for its **strawberries**, dramatic viewpoints over the Western Ghats, the ancient temple at the source of the **Krishna river**, and the Maratha fort of **Pratapgad**. Its smaller neighbour **Panchgani**, known for its boarding schools and Table Land, is just 18 km away.

## How to reach Mahabaleshwar

- **By road:** about 120 km from Pune (3 to 3.5 hours) and about 260 km from Mumbai (6 to 7 hours).
- **By train:** **Satara** and **Wathar** are the nearest stations; Pune has far better connections.
- **By air:** **Pune** Airport is the nearest.

## Viewpoints

Mahabaleshwar has many "points" along the edges of its plateau. Some of the best:

- **Arthur's Seat:** known as the "queen of all points", with deep valleys on both sides.
- **Wilson Point (Sunrise Point):** the highest point in Mahabaleshwar, best at sunrise.
- **Elephant's Head and Needle Hole Point:** a rock formation shaped like an elephant's trunk, with a hole in the rock.
- **Kate's Point:** views over the Dhom and Balkawdi dams.
- **Bombay Point (Sunset Point):** the classic sunset spot.
- **Lodwick Point:** a memorial to General Lodwick, one of the first British officers to reach the plateau.

In the monsoon, many points are wrapped in cloud, and some may be closed.

## Venna Lake

A lake at the edge of town, with **boating**, horse rides and food stalls along the shore. It's a lively evening spot.

## Old Mahabaleshwar

The **Mahabaleshwar Temple** and the **Panchganga Temple** in Old Mahabaleshwar mark the source of the **Krishna** and four other rivers. Water flows from a carved cow's mouth (gomukh) into a tank. The **Krishnabai Temple** nearby overlooks the Krishna valley.

## Pratapgad Fort

About 24 km from Mahabaleshwar, **Pratapgad** was built by **Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj** in the 17th century. It was here, in 1659, that Shivaji defeated Afzal Khan, a turning point in the rise of the Maratha empire. See the Bhavani temple, the fort walls and the statue of Shivaji. Allow a few hours, and wear good shoes.

## Strawberries

Mahabaleshwar produces most of India's strawberries, and **Mahabaleshwar strawberry** has a GI tag.

- **Season:** roughly **November to March**, with the peak around February.
- **Strawberry farms:** pick your own in season.
- **Mapro Garden:** a popular stop between Mahabaleshwar and Panchgani for strawberry cream, jams, crushes and chocolates.
- **Strawberry Festival:** held in some years in the peak season.

## Panchgani

- **Table Land:** a vast, flat laterite plateau, one of the largest in Asia, with views over the Krishna valley. Good for walks and horse rides, and for sunset.
- **Sydney Point and Parsi Point:** viewpoints over the Dhom dam.
- **Devil's Kitchen:** caves linked to the Mahabharata legend.

## Kaas Plateau

About 25 km from Satara, the **Kaas Plateau** is part of the Western Ghats UNESCO World Heritage Site and famous for its **seasonal wildflower bloom**, usually from **late August to early October**. Entry is controlled, with **online booking** and limited numbers each day; stay on marked paths and don't pick the flowers. It can be combined with Mahabaleshwar in the right season.

## What to eat and buy

- **Strawberries with cream**, fresh strawberry juice and jam.
- **Corn pattice** and **vada pav**.
- **Chikki**, fudge and jams from local shops.
- **Maharashtrian thalis** in town.

## Best time to visit

- **October to June:** the main season. **November to March** is strawberry season and has cool, clear weather.
- **Monsoon (June to September):** very heavy rain; many points are closed or covered in cloud, though the waterfalls are spectacular. Late August to early October is the Kaas bloom.

## A 2-day plan

1. **Day 1:** Wilson Point at sunrise, Old Mahabaleshwar temples, Arthur's Seat and Elephant's Head, Venna Lake in the evening.
2. **Day 2:** Pratapgad Fort in the morning, Mapro Garden, Table Land in Panchgani for sunset.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is strawberry season in Mahabaleshwar?

From about November to March, with the peak around February.

### How far is Panchgani from Mahabaleshwar?

About 18 km, around 40 minutes by road.

### Do I need to book to visit Kaas Plateau?

Yes. Entry is controlled during the flowering season, with online booking and daily limits.
