Shirdi, a small town in Maharashtra's Ahmednagar district, was the home of **Sai Baba**, the saint revered by Hindus and Muslims alike for his message of faith (shraddha) and patience (saburi). Millions of devotees visit his **Samadhi Mandir** every year. This guide explains how to plan a smooth darshan and what else to see.

## How to reach Shirdi

- **By air:** **Shirdi Airport** has flights from several cities. **Aurangabad** (about 120 km) and **Pune** (about 185 km) have more options.
- **By train:** **Sainagar Shirdi** station has direct trains from Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Bengaluru and other cities. **Kopargaon** is another nearby station.
- **By road:** about 240 km from Mumbai and 90 km from Nashik.

## Darshan at the Samadhi Mandir

The **Samadhi Mandir** holds Sai Baba's tomb and his marble statue. It is managed by the **Shri Saibaba Sansthan Trust**.

- **Free darshan:** available through the general queue; waits can be long on busy days.
- **Paid darshan and aarti passes:** can be booked on the **official Shri Saibaba Sansthan Trust website**, with a limited number available for each slot. Book early for weekends and festivals, and carry the same ID.
- **Phones, cameras and bags** are not allowed inside; use the lockers and cloakrooms.
- **Dress modestly.**

### The four daily aartis

1. **Kakad Aarti:** the early-morning aarti, before dawn.
2. **Madhyan Aarti:** the midday aarti.
3. **Dhoop Aarti:** at sunset.
4. **Shej Aarti:** the night aarti, before the temple closes.

Timings are published by the Sansthan and can change; check the official site before you go.

## Sites linked to Sai Baba

- **Dwarkamai:** the old mosque where Sai Baba lived. The **dhuni** (sacred fire) he lit still burns, and the stone he sat on is kept here.
- **Chavadi:** where Sai Baba slept on alternate nights; a procession is held here on Thursdays.
- **Gurusthan:** a neem tree under which Sai Baba was first seen as a young man.
- **Lendi Baug:** a garden that Sai Baba watered and tended himself.
- **Sai Heritage Village and Wet n Joy:** attractions near Shirdi popular with families.

## Busiest days

Thursdays are always busy, as are weekends and holidays. The biggest festivals are **Ram Navami**, **Guru Purnima** and **Vijayadashami** (Dussehra), which marks the anniversary of Sai Baba's samadhi in 1918. Book stays and passes well in advance for these.

## Nearby pilgrimages and trips

- **Shani Shingnapur:** about 70 km away, famous for the open-air Shani temple and a village where many houses traditionally had no doors.
- **Nashik and Trimbakeshwar:** about 90 km and 120 km away. Trimbakeshwar is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas, near the source of the Godavari.
- **Aurangabad, Ajanta and Ellora:** Ellora is about 100 km away. See our [Aurangabad, Ajanta and Ellora guide](/blog/aurangabad-ajanta-ellora-guide).

## Where to stay and eat

Shirdi has options from Sansthan-run accommodation (bookable on the official site) to many private hotels near the temple. The Sansthan's **prasadalaya** serves simple, low-cost vegetarian meals to thousands of devotees every day.

## Best time to visit

Shirdi is visited year-round. **October to March** has the most pleasant weather. Summers are hot, and the monsoon is moderate.

## A 2-day plan

1. **Day 1:** arrive, evening Dhoop Aarti, darshan, and Dwarkamai and Chavadi.
2. **Day 2:** Kakad Aarti at dawn, Gurusthan and Lendi Baug, then Shani Shingnapur in the afternoon.

## Frequently asked questions

### How can I book darshan or aarti passes in Shirdi?

On the official Shri Saibaba Sansthan Trust website. Book early and carry the same ID.

### How much time does darshan take in Shirdi?

On a normal weekday, one to three hours in the general queue. On Thursdays, weekends and festivals it can take much longer.

### How far is Shani Shingnapur from Shirdi?

About 70 km, roughly 1.5 hours by road.
