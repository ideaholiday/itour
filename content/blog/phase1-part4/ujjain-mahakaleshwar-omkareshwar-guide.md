**Ujjain**, on the banks of the Shipra river in Madhya Pradesh, is one of the seven sacred cities of Hinduism and home to the **Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga**, famous for its dawn **Bhasma Aarti**. About 140 km away, **Omkareshwar** sits on an island in the Narmada shaped like the sacred syllable Om, and is home to another of the twelve Jyotirlingas. Together they make one of the most rewarding pilgrim trips in central India.

## How to reach Ujjain

- **By air:** **Indore**'s Devi Ahilya Bai Holkar Airport is about 55 km from Ujjain, roughly 1.5 hours by road.
- **By train:** **Ujjain Junction** has direct trains from Delhi, Mumbai, Bhopal and many other cities.
- **By road:** Indore is about 55 km away and Bhopal about 190 km.

## Mahakaleshwar Temple

Mahakaleshwar is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Shiva, and its lingam is believed to be **dakshinamukhi** (south-facing), which gives it special significance.

### The Bhasma Aarti

The **Bhasma Aarti**, performed before dawn, is the temple's most famous ritual, when the lingam is adorned with sacred ash.

- **Booking:** passes for the Bhasma Aarti are booked through the **official Shri Mahakaleshwar temple website**, and fill up fast. Book as early as possible, and carry the same photo ID.
- **Timing:** it takes place very early in the morning, well before sunrise. Check the reporting time on your pass.
- **Dress code:** for close participation, men are usually required to wear a **dhoti** and women a **saree**. Check the current rules when you book.
- **Phones and bags** are not allowed inside; use the cloakroom.

If you can't get a Bhasma Aarti pass, you can still have darshan during the day through general or paid quick-darshan queues.

### Shri Mahakal Lok

The **Mahakal Lok** corridor, opened in 2022, is a large walkway around the temple with murals, statues and stories from Shiva legends, and it is beautifully lit at night.

## Other temples in Ujjain

- **Kal Bhairav Temple:** famous for offerings of liquor to the deity.
- **Harsiddhi Temple:** one of the Shakti Peethas, with two tall lamp towers that are lit during festivals.
- **Ram Ghat:** the main ghat on the Shipra, with an evening aarti.
- **Sandipani Ashram:** where Krishna, Balarama and Sudama are said to have studied.
- **Mangalnath Temple:** believed to be the birthplace of Mars in Hindu astrology.
- **Chintaman Ganesh Temple:** an old Ganesha temple outside the city.
- **Vedh Shala (Jantar Mantar):** an 18th-century observatory built by Jai Singh II.

Ujjain hosts the **Simhastha Kumbh Mela** every 12 years; the next one is expected in **2028**.

## Omkareshwar

**Omkareshwar** is about 140 km from Ujjain (and about 80 km from Indore).

- **Omkareshwar Jyotirlinga:** on **Mandhata island** in the Narmada, reached by a bridge or boat.
- **Mamleshwar Temple:** on the south bank, also considered part of the Jyotirlinga.
- **Statue of Oneness:** a 108-foot statue of **Adi Shankaracharya** on the island, unveiled in 2023.
- **Parikrama:** a walk around the island passes old temples and ghats.
- **Boat ride** on the Narmada at sunset.

## Maheshwar

About 65 km from Omkareshwar, **Maheshwar** was the capital of Queen **Ahilyabai Holkar**. See the fort and palace overlooking the Narmada, the ghats with their temples, and the handloom **Maheshwari sarees** woven in the town.

## A 3-day plan

1. **Day 1:** arrive in Ujjain; Kal Bhairav, Harsiddhi and Mahakal Lok; evening aarti at Ram Ghat.
2. **Day 2:** **Bhasma Aarti** at dawn, Mahakaleshwar darshan, Sandipani Ashram and Mangalnath; drive to Omkareshwar.
3. **Day 3:** Omkareshwar and Mamleshwar darshan, the Statue of Oneness, then Maheshwar or Indore.

## Food in Indore and Ujjain

Stop in **Indore**, one of India's great street-food cities: **poha-jalebi** for breakfast, and the evening food streets of **Sarafa Bazaar** and **Chappan Dukan** for garadu, bhutte ka kees and many sweets.

## Best time to visit

**October to March** is the most comfortable. **Mahashivratri** (February or March) and the month of **Shravan** (July or August) are the busiest times at Mahakaleshwar. Summers are very hot.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I book the Bhasma Aarti at Mahakaleshwar?

On the official Shri Mahakaleshwar temple website. Book early, carry the same ID, and follow the dress code.

### How far is Omkareshwar from Ujjain?

About 140 km, around 3 to 3.5 hours by road.

### Can I visit both Jyotirlingas in one day?

It is possible but tiring. Two to three days lets you do both, and Maheshwar, comfortably.
