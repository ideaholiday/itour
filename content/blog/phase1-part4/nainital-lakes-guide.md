Nainital, in the Kumaon hills of Uttarakhand, is built around a green, eye-shaped lake surrounded by forested peaks. It was a summer retreat of the British, and it is still one of the most popular hill stations in North India, with boating on the lake, a lively Mall Road, colonial buildings and easy trips to the quieter lakes of Bhimtal, Sattal and Naukuchiatal.

## How to reach Nainital

- **By train:** **Kathgodam** is the nearest railway station, about 35 km away, with direct trains from Delhi, including overnight options.
- **By air:** **Pantnagar** Airport is about 70 km away.
- **By road:** Delhi is about 300 km away, around 7 to 8 hours by road.

**Traffic and parking:** in peak season and on long weekends, Nainital gets very crowded and vehicle entry and parking can be restricted. Consider parking at the designated lots, using shuttles, and arriving early.

## Things to do in Nainital

### Naini Lake

The lake is the heart of the town. Hire a **rowing boat, pedal boat or sailing yacht**, or take a slow walk around it. The northern end is **Mallital** and the southern end is **Tallital**, linked by the **Mall Road**.

### Naina Devi Temple

On the northern shore of the lake, the temple is one of the Shakti Peethas and gives the town its name.

### The ropeway and Snow View Point

A cable car climbs from near the Mall Road to **Snow View Point**, with views of the Himalayan peaks on clear days, including Nanda Devi.

### Naina Peak (China Peak)

The highest point around Nainital, at about 2,600 metres, reached by a trek or a pony ride. On clear mornings the views of the snow range are superb.

### Tiffin Top (Dorothy's Seat)

A viewpoint reached by a walk through the forest, with views over the lake and the hills.

### Other sights

- **Eco Cave Gardens:** interconnected small caves and gardens, fun for children.
- **St John in the Wilderness:** a 19th-century church in the forest.
- **Raj Bhavan:** the Governor's residence, a Victorian Gothic building with a golf course; guided visits are sometimes allowed.
- **Mall Road:** shopping for woollens, candles and local snacks.

## Day trips from Nainital

- **Bhimtal:** a larger, quieter lake with an island aquarium, about 22 km away.
- **Naukuchiatal:** the "lake of nine corners", good for paragliding and boating.
- **Sattal:** a cluster of seven lakes in dense forest, a birdwatcher's favourite.
- **Mukteshwar:** about 50 km away, with an old Shiva temple, orchards and views of the Himalaya from the cliffs of **Chauli ki Jali**.
- **Kainchi Dham:** the ashram of Neem Karoli Baba, about 17 km away, which draws large crowds, especially on its foundation day in June.

## What to eat

Try Kumaoni food: **bhatt ki churkani** (black soybean curry), **aloo ke gutke**, **ras bhaat** and **bal mithai**, the brown fudge sweet coated in white sugar balls. The Mall Road has plenty of cafés and street snacks.

## Best time to visit

- **March to June:** pleasant weather and the peak season. Expect crowds and high prices.
- **July to September:** monsoon greenery, but landslides and heavy rain.
- **October and November:** clear skies and the best Himalayan views.
- **December to February:** cold, with occasional snow.

## A 3-day plan

1. **Day 1:** arrive, boat on Naini Lake, Naina Devi Temple and the Mall Road.
2. **Day 2:** ropeway to Snow View, Tiffin Top walk, and Naina Peak on a clear morning.
3. **Day 3:** Bhimtal, Naukuchiatal and Sattal, or Mukteshwar.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many days are enough for Nainital?

Two to three days covers Nainital and the nearby lakes.

### What is the nearest railway station to Nainital?

Kathgodam, about 35 km away.

### When can you see snow in Nainital?

Occasionally between December and February, though snowfall varies from year to year.
