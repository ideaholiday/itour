Long before Hampi, the **Chalukya** kings ruled much of the Deccan from **Badami** (ancient Vatapi) in northern Karnataka, between the 6th and 8th centuries. They left behind rock-cut cave temples, sandstone shrines and experiments in temple design that shaped Indian architecture for centuries. The three sites of **Badami, Aihole and Pattadakal** are close together and make a rewarding two-day trip.

## How to reach Badami

- **By train:** **Badami** station is about 5 km from town, with trains from Hubballi, Bengaluru and other cities.
- **By air:** **Hubballi** Airport is about 110 km away, and Belagavi is another option.
- **By road:** Hampi is about 150 km away, around 3.5 hours by road.

Stay in **Badami**, which has the most hotels, and visit Aihole and Pattadakal as a day trip.

## Badami

### The cave temples

Four cave temples are carved into the red sandstone cliff above **Agastya Lake**, dating from around the 6th century.

- **Cave 1:** dedicated to Shiva, with a dancing Nataraja with 18 arms.
- **Cave 2:** dedicated to Vishnu, with Trivikrama and Varaha panels.
- **Cave 3:** the largest and finest, dedicated to Vishnu, with superb carvings of Vishnu on the serpent Shesha and as Narasimha.
- **Cave 4:** a Jain cave, with carvings of the Tirthankaras.

Climb the steps between the caves for views over the lake and the town.

### Agastya Lake and the Bhutanatha temples

The green lake at the foot of the cliffs is lined with old temples. The **Bhutanatha temples** on the eastern shore are especially beautiful at sunset.

### The North Fort

A climb through a narrow sandstone gorge leads to the **North Fort**, with old temples, granaries and wide views. Go early, when it is cooler.

**Tip:** monkeys are everywhere around the caves. Don't carry food in your hands.

## Pattadakal

About 22 km from Badami, **Pattadakal** is a **UNESCO World Heritage Site** where Chalukya kings were crowned. Its group of 8th-century temples, set on the banks of the Malaprabha river, blends the **northern (Nagara)** and **southern (Dravida)** temple styles side by side.

The highlight is the **Virupaksha Temple**, built by Queen Lokamahadevi to celebrate a victory, with rich carvings of the Ramayana and Mahabharata. The **Mallikarjuna Temple** beside it is similar in style. Hire an approved guide at the gate to understand the details.

## Aihole

About 35 km from Badami, **Aihole** has been called the "cradle of Indian temple architecture". More than 100 temples, from the 5th century onwards, are scattered around the village, showing how Indian temple design developed.

- **Durga Temple:** the most famous, with an unusual apsidal (horseshoe-shaped) plan and a colonnaded walkway.
- **Lad Khan Temple:** one of the oldest, originally built like an assembly hall.
- **Ravanaphadi Cave:** a rock-cut Shiva temple with fine sculptures.
- **Meguti Jain Temple:** on a hill, with the famous Aihole inscription and views over the village.

## Mahakuta

Between Badami and Pattadakal, the **Mahakuta** temple complex has Chalukya temples around a sacred spring-fed tank where pilgrims bathe.

## A 2-day plan

1. **Day 1:** Badami cave temples in the morning, the North Fort, and Agastya Lake and the Bhutanatha temples at sunset.
2. **Day 2:** Aihole in the morning, Pattadakal in the afternoon, and Mahakuta on the way back.

## Best time to visit

**October to February** is the best time, with warm days and cool mornings. The monsoon (June to September) turns the landscape green but can be wet. **March to May** is very hot, so start early and rest at midday.

## What to eat

North Karnataka food is hearty and vegetarian-friendly: try **jolada rotti** (jowar flatbread) with **ennegai** (stuffed aubergine curry), **kharabath**, and **girmit**, a spicy puffed-rice snack.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many days do I need for Badami, Aihole and Pattadakal?

Two days is enough to see all three at a comfortable pace.

### Is Pattadakal a UNESCO World Heritage Site?

Yes. The group of monuments at Pattadakal is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

### How far is Badami from Hampi?

About 150 km, roughly 3.5 hours by road.
