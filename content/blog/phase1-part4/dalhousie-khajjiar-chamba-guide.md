Dalhousie, spread over five hills in western Himachal Pradesh, is a quiet colonial hill town of old churches, pine-lined walks and views of the Dhauladhar and Pir Panjal ranges. Its best-known neighbour, **Khajjiar**, is a saucer-shaped meadow ringed by deodar forest, often called the "Mini Switzerland of India". Together with the old town of **Chamba**, they make a peaceful mountain escape away from the busier hill stations.

## How to reach Dalhousie

- **By train:** **Pathankot** is the nearest major railway station, about 80 km away, roughly 3 hours by road.
- **By air:** Gaggal (Kangra) Airport near Dharamshala is about 3.5 hours away; Amritsar is another option.
- **By road:** Buses and cabs run from Pathankot, Dharamshala, Amritsar and Delhi.

## Dalhousie

The town was founded by the British in the 1850s and named after Lord Dalhousie. Much of its charm is in walking.

- **Gandhi Chowk:** the town centre, with the Mall, cafés and **St John's Church**, one of the oldest churches in town.
- **Subhash Chowk:** another square with a church and shops, connected to Gandhi Chowk by gentle walks.
- **Thandi Sadak and Garam Sadak:** the "cold road" and "warm road", named for how much sun they get; both are lovely walks.
- **Panchpula:** a picnic spot with streams, small waterfalls and a memorial to freedom fighter Sardar Ajit Singh.
- **Satdhara Falls:** springs believed to have healing properties.
- **Tibetan market:** for woollens and handicrafts.

### Dainkund Peak

The highest point around Dalhousie, at about 2,750 metres, with views of the snow ranges. A short walk leads to the **Pohlani Mata Temple**. It is often called the "singing hill" for the sound of the wind in the trees.

## Kalatop Wildlife Sanctuary

Between Dalhousie and Khajjiar, **Kalatop** is a dense forest of deodar and pine with walking trails and a forest rest house. You may see barking deer, Himalayan black bear signs and many birds. Entry for vehicles is controlled; walking in is a lovely option.

## Khajjiar

About 24 km from Dalhousie, **Khajjiar** is a wide green meadow at about 1,900 metres, with a small lake in the middle and dense cedar forest around it. It is perfect for walks, picnics and horse rides.

- **Khajji Nag Temple:** a 12th-century temple with wooden carvings, dedicated to the serpent god.
- **Adventure activities:** zorbing, paragliding and horse riding are offered in season; choose registered operators.
- **Snow:** in winter, the meadow is often covered in snow.

Go early to enjoy the meadow before the day-trip crowds arrive.

## Chamba

About 50 km from Dalhousie, **Chamba** is an old princely town on the banks of the Ravi, over a thousand years old.

- **Lakshmi Narayan Temple complex:** a group of stone temples in the shikhara style, some dating back to the 10th century.
- **Chaugan:** a large grassy ground at the heart of the town, where the **Minjar Fair** is held every July or August.
- **Bhuri Singh Museum:** Chamba paintings, murals and the famous **Chamba rumal** embroidery.
- **Chamunda Devi Temple:** a hilltop temple with views over the town.

## Best time to visit

- **March to June:** pleasant weather, the most popular season.
- **July to September:** monsoon greenery, but landslides and heavy rain.
- **October and November:** clear skies and crisp views.
- **December to February:** snow in Dalhousie and Khajjiar, beautiful but cold, and roads can close after heavy snowfall.

## A 3-day plan

1. **Day 1:** arrive in Dalhousie; Gandhi Chowk, St John's Church and the Thandi Sadak walk.
2. **Day 2:** Dainkund Peak in the morning, Kalatop forest, and Khajjiar in the afternoon.
3. **Day 3:** day trip to Chamba, or Panchpula and Satdhara before leaving.

## Frequently asked questions

### How far is Khajjiar from Dalhousie?

About 24 km, roughly an hour by road.

### Does it snow in Dalhousie?

Yes, usually between December and February. Check road conditions after heavy snowfall.

### How many days are enough for Dalhousie and Khajjiar?

Two to three days, plus a day for Chamba.
