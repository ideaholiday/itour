**McLeod Ganj**, above Dharamshala in Himachal Pradesh, is the home in exile of the **Dalai Lama** and the Tibetan government, and has become one of the most distinctive towns in India. Prayer flags flutter over narrow streets, monks in maroon robes walk past cafés and bookshops, and the snow-capped **Dhauladhar range** rises directly behind the town. It is also the starting point for **Triund**, one of the most popular short treks in the Himalaya.

## How to reach Dharamshala

- **By air:** **Gaggal (Kangra) Airport** is about 15 km from Dharamshala and 20 km from McLeod Ganj.
- **By train:** **Pathankot** is the nearest broad-gauge station, about 85 km away. The narrow-gauge Kangra Valley Railway also runs through the region.
- **By road:** overnight buses run from Delhi (about 480 km). Dharamshala is about 3 hours from Pathankot.

Lower Dharamshala and McLeod Ganj are about 10 km apart by road; a ropeway also connects them.

## McLeod Ganj

### Tsuglagkhang Complex

The main temple of the Tibetan community and the residence of the Dalai Lama, with the **Namgyal Monastery** and the **Tibet Museum**, which tells the story of Tibet and the exile. Walk the **kora** (circumambulation path) around the complex through the forest, lined with prayer wheels and flags. When the Dalai Lama gives public teachings, the complex fills with pilgrims; check the official schedule.

### Tibetan culture

- **Library of Tibetan Works and Archives** in lower Dharamshala: manuscripts, a museum and courses in Buddhist philosophy.
- **Tibetan Children's Village** and **Tibetan Institute of Performing Arts:** learn about education and the preservation of Tibetan culture.
- **Momo-making and cooking classes**, Tibetan language and meditation courses.

### Bhagsu Nag Temple and Waterfall

An old Shiva temple with a spring-fed pool, and a short walk beyond it to the **Bhagsu waterfall**. The trail continues to the **Shiva Café** above the falls.

### Dharamkot

A quiet village above McLeod Ganj with yoga and meditation centres, including the Tushita Meditation Centre, and relaxed cafés.

### St John in the Wilderness

A neo-Gothic church from the 1850s in a deodar forest, with Belgian stained-glass windows. **Lord Elgin**, a Viceroy of India, is buried here.

## The Triund trek

**Triund** is a grassy ridge at about 2,850 metres with sweeping views of the Dhauladhar peaks on one side and the Kangra valley on the other.

- **Distance:** about 9 km one way from McLeod Ganj, or about 7 km from **Galu Devi Temple** above Dharamkot, where most people start.
- **Difficulty:** easy to moderate, with the last stretch of about 22 switchbacks being steep.
- **Time:** 3 to 5 hours up, depending on fitness.
- **Staying overnight:** camping and stays at the top are regulated and rules change, so check with the forest department or a registered operator before planning a night on the ridge.
- **Extension:** fit trekkers continue to the **Snowline** and **Lahesh Cave**, and experienced trekkers cross the **Indrahar Pass** with a guide.

**Tips:** start early, carry water and a rain jacket, and avoid the trek in heavy rain or snow without a guide.

## Dharamshala

- **HPCA Cricket Stadium:** one of the most scenic cricket grounds in the world, with the Dhauladhars behind it.
- **War Memorial:** a memorial to soldiers from Himachal in a pine forest.
- **Kangra Fort:** a large ancient fort about 20 km away.
- **Norbulingka Institute:** about 10 km away, a centre for Tibetan arts with beautiful gardens, workshops and a temple.
- **Tea gardens:** walk through the tea estates around Dharamshala, and visit a tea factory.

## Best time to visit

- **March to June:** pleasant weather, the best time for Triund.
- **July to September:** heavy monsoon rain; treks can be slippery and landslides happen.
- **October and November:** clear skies and the best mountain views.
- **December to February:** cold, with snow at higher levels and occasionally in McLeod Ganj.

## A 3-day plan

1. **Day 1:** Tsuglagkhang, the Tibet Museum and the kora; evening in McLeod Ganj's cafés.
2. **Day 2:** Triund trek, down by late afternoon.
3. **Day 3:** Bhagsu waterfall, St John in the Wilderness, the cricket stadium and Norbulingka.

## Frequently asked questions

### How difficult is the Triund trek?

It is easy to moderate. Most reasonably fit people manage it in 3 to 5 hours, with a steep final section.

### Can I meet the Dalai Lama?

Private meetings are rare, but you may be able to attend public teachings when they are held. Check the official schedule.

### How many days are enough for McLeod Ganj?

Three days covers the town, the Triund trek and Dharamshala's sights.
