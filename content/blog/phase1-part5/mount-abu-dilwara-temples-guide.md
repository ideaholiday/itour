**Mount Abu**, at about 1,200 metres in the Aravalli hills, is **Rajasthan's only hill station** and a cool escape from the desert heat. It is best known for the **Dilwara Temples**, some of the most exquisitely carved marble temples in the world, and for the green **Nakki Lake**, forested hills and viewpoints over the plains of Rajasthan and Gujarat.

## How to reach Mount Abu

- **By train:** **Abu Road** station, about 28 km away at the foot of the hills, has direct trains from Delhi, Mumbai, Ahmedabad and Jaipur.
- **By air:** **Udaipur** Airport is about 165 km away, roughly 3.5 hours by road. Ahmedabad is another option.
- **By road:** Udaipur is about 165 km away and Ahmedabad about 220 km.

Mount Abu fits well into a Rajasthan trip with Udaipur; see our [Udaipur guide](/blog/udaipur-city-of-lakes-guide).

## The Dilwara Temples

The **Dilwara Temples** are a group of five Jain temples built between the **11th and 13th centuries**. From the outside they look simple, but inside, the **white marble carving** is breathtakingly fine: ceilings, pillars, doorways and arches carved like lace.

- **Vimal Vasahi:** the oldest, dedicated to Adinath, the first Tirthankara, with an extraordinary carved dome.
- **Luna Vasahi:** dedicated to Neminath, with some of the finest and most delicate carving of all.
- **Pittalhar, Parshvanath (Khartar Vasahi)** and **Mahavir Swami** temples complete the group.

**Visiting rules:**

- **Non-Jain visitors** are usually allowed in during set hours around midday; check the current timings.
- **Photography is not allowed** inside, and phones and cameras may need to be deposited.
- **Leather items** are not allowed, and modest dress is required.
- Guided tours are often available at the entrance and help you understand the carvings.

## Nakki Lake

A lake at the heart of Mount Abu, surrounded by hills and odd-shaped rocks such as **Toad Rock**. Take a **boat ride**, walk around the lake, and browse the market along the shore. According to legend, the lake was dug by the gods with their nails (nakh).

## Viewpoints

- **Sunset Point:** the most popular spot to watch the sun go down over the plains.
- **Honeymoon Point (Anadra Point):** views over the valley, also good at sunset.
- **Toad Rock:** a short climb from Nakki Lake to a rock shaped like a toad, with views over the lake.

## Guru Shikhar

About 15 km from town, **Guru Shikhar** is the **highest peak in the Aravalli range**, at about 1,720 metres. A short climb of steps leads to the **Dattatreya temple** at the top, with sweeping views. There is also an old bell and a Mount Abu observatory nearby.

## Other places to see

- **Achalgarh Fort and Achaleshwar Mahadev Temple:** a fort built by Rana Kumbha and a temple with Shiva's toe print and a large brass Nandi.
- **Arbuda Devi (Adhar Devi) Temple:** a cave temple reached by a climb of several hundred steps.
- **Brahma Kumaris World Spiritual University:** the headquarters of the Brahma Kumaris, with a museum and meditation sessions.
- **Mount Abu Wildlife Sanctuary:** forests with sloth bears, leopards and many birds; jeep safaris and nature walks are available.
- **Trevor's Tank:** a crocodile breeding spot and picnic area in the forest.

## Best time to visit

- **October to March:** cool, pleasant weather; the most comfortable time.
- **April to June:** warm, but much cooler than the plains; the peak summer holiday season.
- **July to September:** monsoon greenery and mist, with occasional heavy rain.

The **Winter Festival** is held around late December, and the **Summer Festival** around Buddha Purnima, with folk music and dance.

## A 2-day plan

1. **Day 1:** Dilwara Temples, Nakki Lake and Toad Rock, and Sunset Point in the evening.
2. **Day 2:** Guru Shikhar in the morning, Achalgarh and Arbuda Devi Temple, and the Brahma Kumaris museum.

## What to eat

Try **Rajasthani** and **Gujarati thalis**, **dal baati churma**, **rabri**, and hot snacks from the stalls around Nakki Lake.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can non-Jains visit the Dilwara Temples?

Yes, usually during set hours around midday. Photography is not allowed inside.

### How far is Mount Abu from Udaipur?

About 165 km, roughly 3.5 hours by road.

### Is Mount Abu worth visiting in summer?

Yes. It is much cooler than the rest of Rajasthan, though busy during the summer holidays.
