The shrine of **Mata Vaishno Devi**, in a cave high in the **Trikuta hills** of Jammu and Kashmir, is one of the most visited pilgrimages in India. Every year, millions of devotees walk from the base town of **Katra** to the Bhawan to have darshan of the three natural rock formations (pindis) representing Maha Kali, Maha Lakshmi and Maha Saraswati. The yatra is open all year and is well organised by the **Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Shrine Board**.

## How to reach Katra

- **By train:** **Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Katra** station has direct trains from Delhi, including the Vande Bharat, and many other cities.
- **By air:** **Jammu** Airport is about 50 km away, roughly 1.5 to 2 hours by road.
- **By road:** Jammu to Katra is about 50 km; buses and taxis run all day.

## Yatra registration

**Registration is mandatory** for every pilgrim. Register online on the **official Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Shrine Board website** before you travel, or at the registration counters in Katra. You receive an **RFID yatra card**, which you must carry and scan at the start of the trek. Register close to your start time, as cards are valid only for a limited period.

## The trek from Katra

The trek starts at **Banganga**, near Katra, and the main route to the Bhawan is about **13 km**, climbing to around 1,500 metres above Katra. The path is paved, lit at night, and lined with shelters, food stalls, medical posts and drinking water.

- **Banganga:** the starting point, where pilgrims bathe in the stream.
- **Charan Paduka:** a temple with the footprints of the Mata.
- **Adhkuwari (Ardhkuwari):** the halfway point, with the **Garbh Joon** cave, where the Mata is said to have meditated for nine months. Entry to the cave is by token and queue.
- **Himkoti and Sanjichhat:** on the way to the Bhawan.

**The new route:** from Adhkuwari, a newer, wider and less steep track via **Himkoti** leads to the Bhawan. It has **battery cars** for senior citizens and people with disabilities, booked at the counters.

Most walkers take **5 to 7 hours** to reach the Bhawan and a little less to come back. Many pilgrims walk at night to avoid the heat and crowds.

## Other ways to reach the Bhawan

- **Ponies, palkis and pithus:** available at Katra and along the route, at rates fixed by the Shrine Board. Book at official counters.
- **Helicopter:** a service runs between **Katra and Sanjichhat**, about 2.5 km from the Bhawan. Book only on the **official Shrine Board website**; beware of fake booking sites and agents.
- **Ropeway to Bhairon:** from the Bhawan to the **Bhairon Temple**.

## Darshan at the Bhawan

- Deposit your phone, belts, bags and leather items at the **cloakrooms** before darshan; they are not allowed inside.
- Queues can be long on weekends and during **Navratri**. The Shrine Board announces the current waiting time at the Bhawan.
- The **Atka Aarti**, held in the morning and evening, can be attended with a pass.

## Bhairon Temple

According to tradition, the yatra is complete only after visiting the **Bhairon Temple**, about 1.5 km above the Bhawan, where the demon Bhairon is said to have asked for forgiveness. Reach it by a steep walk or by the **ropeway** from the Bhawan.

## Where to stay

- **Katra:** many hotels at all budgets, and Shrine Board accommodation.
- **Along the route and at the Bhawan:** Shrine Board dormitories and rooms, booked online in advance.

## Best time to visit

The yatra is open all year.

- **March to June:** pleasant weather, busy in the summer holidays.
- **September to October:** clear skies; **Navratri** brings very large crowds.
- **July and August:** monsoon rain, slippery paths and occasional landslides.
- **December to February:** cold, with snow on the upper stretches at times; fewer crowds.

## Tips for the yatra

- Carry a light jacket even in summer, and warm clothes in winter.
- Wear comfortable walking shoes.
- Carry a small torch, water and personal medicines.
- Keep your yatra card and ID safe; you'll need them on the way.
- Senior citizens should consult a doctor before trekking and consider the battery cars or helicopter.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is the Vaishno Devi trek?

About 13 km one way from Katra to the Bhawan, plus about 1.5 km more to the Bhairon Temple.

### Is registration mandatory for Vaishno Devi?

Yes. Every pilgrim must register and carry an RFID yatra card.

### How do I book a helicopter for Vaishno Devi?

Only on the official Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Shrine Board website. Avoid other sites and agents.
