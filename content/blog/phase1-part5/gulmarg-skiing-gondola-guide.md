**Gulmarg**, a high meadow about 50 km from Srinagar in Kashmir, is India's best-known ski resort. In winter, deep powder snow covers its slopes, and the **Gulmarg Gondola** carries skiers high up towards **Apharwat Peak**, giving access to some of the longest ski runs in Asia. Whether you are a complete beginner or an experienced off-piste skier, Gulmarg offers something special.

## How to reach Gulmarg

- **Fly to Srinagar**, which has direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai and other cities.
- **Drive to Gulmarg**, about 50 km and 1.5 to 2 hours, via Tangmarg. In heavy snow, vehicles may need chains, and local rules sometimes require switching to a local vehicle at Tangmarg.

## The ski season

- **The main season runs from about December to March**, depending on snowfall.
- **January and February** usually have the best snow conditions.
- **March** often brings sunny days and spring snow.

Check snow reports and road status before you travel.

## The Gulmarg Gondola

The **Gulmarg Gondola** is one of the highest cable cars in the world and the backbone of skiing here.

- **Phase 1:** from Gulmarg to **Kongdori**, with intermediate slopes and ski runs back to Gulmarg.
- **Phase 2:** from Kongdori towards the **Apharwat** ridge at nearly 4,000 metres, with long, steep and off-piste runs for advanced and expert skiers.

Tickets are sold by the **Jammu and Kashmir Cable Car Corporation**, including online. In peak season, book in advance and arrive early, as queues can be long. Phase 2 may close in bad weather or high avalanche risk.

## Beginner slopes and ski lifts

- **Beginners** learn on the gentle slopes around Gulmarg village, served by short lifts and a chairlift.
- **Kongdori** has intermediate runs.
- **Apharwat** is for advanced and expert skiers only.

## Ski schools and courses

- **Indian Institute of Skiing and Mountaineering (IISM):** a government-run institute in Gulmarg that offers structured ski and snowboard courses of one to several weeks, with equipment and accommodation. Apply well in advance.
- **Private ski schools and instructors:** offer lessons by the hour or day. Choose certified instructors with good reviews.
- **Snowboarding** lessons are also available.

A beginner can usually manage basic turns on easy slopes after three to five days of lessons.

## Renting gear

Ski and snowboard equipment, boots, helmets and jackets can be rented in Gulmarg from the ski shops and some hotels. Check that boots fit well and that bindings are adjusted by a technician. **Always wear a helmet.**

## Off-piste safety

Gulmarg's famous powder and off-piste terrain come with **avalanche risk**.

- **Never ski off-piste without a qualified local guide** who knows the conditions.
- Carry an **avalanche transceiver, probe and shovel**, and know how to use them.
- Check the daily **avalanche bulletin** and the ski patrol's advice.
- Don't ski alone, and respect closed areas.

## For non-skiers

- **Gondola rides** for the views over the snowy peaks and valleys.
- **Sledge rides** pulled by locals; agree the price first.
- **Snowmobile rides** and snow biking.
- **St Mary's Church** and the Maharaja's palace in Gulmarg.
- Walks in the snow-covered pine forests.

## Where to stay

Gulmarg has everything from simple guesthouses to luxury hotels, many of which are fully booked in peak season. Book early for January and February, and around Christmas and New Year. Check that your hotel has reliable heating.

## What to wear

- Warm base layers, fleece, and a waterproof ski jacket and pants.
- Gloves, a warm hat, a neck gaiter and goggles or sunglasses.
- Sunscreen; the sun is strong at altitude.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the best time for skiing in Gulmarg?

January and February usually have the best snow. The season runs from about December to March.

### Can beginners ski in Gulmarg?

Yes. There are gentle beginner slopes, ski schools, and IISM courses for first-timers.

### Do I need to book the Gulmarg Gondola in advance?

In peak season, yes. Book online through the official Cable Car Corporation and arrive early.
