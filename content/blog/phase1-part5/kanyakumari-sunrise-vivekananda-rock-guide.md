**Kanyakumari**, at the southern tip of mainland India, is where the **Arabian Sea, the Bay of Bengal and the Indian Ocean** meet. Pilgrims come to bathe at the confluence and visit the **Kumari Amman Temple**, and travellers come for the sunrise and sunset over the sea, the **Vivekananda Rock Memorial** and the towering **Thiruvalluvar Statue** offshore.

## How to reach Kanyakumari

- **By train:** Kanyakumari station has direct trains from many cities, including long-distance trains from Delhi, Mumbai and Chennai. Nagercoil, about 20 km away, has more connections.
- **By air:** **Thiruvananthapuram** International Airport is about 90 km away, around 2.5 hours by road. Madurai is another option.
- **By road:** Thiruvananthapuram is about 90 km away.

## Sunrise and sunset

Kanyakumari is one of the few places in India where you can watch the sun both rise and set over the sea.

- **Sunrise:** crowds gather on the beach and at viewpoints near the temple before dawn.
- **Sunset:** the **Sunset Point** a little west of town is the classic spot.
- **Chitra Pournami:** on the full moon in April or May, you can watch the sun set and the moon rise at about the same time.

## Vivekananda Rock Memorial

About 500 metres offshore, the **Vivekananda Rock Memorial** was built in 1970 on the rock where **Swami Vivekananda** meditated in 1892. It has a meditation hall and the **Shripada Mandapam**, marking a footprint believed to be of the goddess Kumari.

**Ferry:** boats run from the jetty near the temple, operated by the state's Poompuhar Shipping Corporation. Queues can be long; arrive early. Ferries may stop in rough seas.

## Thiruvalluvar Statue

Next to the memorial stands the **133-foot statue** of **Thiruvalluvar**, the Tamil poet-saint who wrote the Thirukkural. Its height represents the 133 chapters of that work. A **glass bridge** now links the statue with the Vivekananda Rock Memorial, so you can walk between them.

## Kumari Amman Temple

The **Kumari Amman Temple** is dedicated to the goddess **Kanyakumari**, a virgin form of Parvati who, according to legend, waits here to marry Shiva. The temple is about 3,000 years old in tradition. Men usually have to remove their shirts to enter, and phones are not allowed inside.

## Other places to see

- **Triveni Sangam:** the bathing ghat at the meeting point of the three seas.
- **Gandhi Mandapam:** built where Mahatma Gandhi's ashes were kept before immersion. On his birthday, 2 October, sunlight falls through a hole in the roof onto the spot.
- **Kamarajar Mani Mandapam:** a memorial to the freedom fighter K. Kamaraj.
- **Our Lady of Ransom Church:** a Gothic church near the beach.
- **Wax museum** and **Baywatch** water park for families.

## Day trips from Kanyakumari

- **Suchindram:** about 12 km away, the **Thanumalayan Temple** dedicated to the trinity of Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva, with musical pillars and a large Hanuman statue.
- **Padmanabhapuram Palace:** about 35 km away, the former palace of the Travancore kings and one of the finest examples of traditional Kerala wooden architecture, though it lies in Tamil Nadu.
- **Vattakottai Fort:** a seaside fort about 7 km away.
- **Thiruvananthapuram and Kovalam:** combine with a trip to Kerala.

Many pilgrims combine Kanyakumari with **Rameswaram** and **Madurai**; see our [Rameswaram guide](/blog/rameswaram-dhanushkodi-guide) and [Madurai guide](/blog/madurai-meenakshi-temple-guide).

## Best time to visit

**October to March** is the most pleasant. Summers are hot and humid, and the monsoon brings rain, especially from October to December, when clouds may block the sunrise.

## A 2-day plan

1. **Day 1:** sunrise, Kumari Amman Temple and the Triveni Sangam, then the ferry to Vivekananda Rock and the Thiruvalluvar Statue; sunset at Sunset Point.
2. **Day 2:** Suchindram and Padmanabhapuram Palace, and Gandhi Mandapam in the evening.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get to Vivekananda Rock Memorial?

By ferry from the jetty near the Kumari Amman Temple. Queues can be long, so arrive early.

### Can you see both sunrise and sunset in Kanyakumari?

Yes, both over the sea, and on Chitra Pournami, the sunset and moonrise at about the same time.

### How many days are enough for Kanyakumari?

One to two days covers the town and nearby sights.
