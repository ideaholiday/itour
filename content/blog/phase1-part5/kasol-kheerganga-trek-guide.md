The **Parvati Valley** in Himachal Pradesh is a narrow, dramatic valley of pine forests, apple orchards and roaring river, with snow peaks at its head. **Kasol**, a small riverside town, is the base for exploring it: cafés by the water, walks to old villages, the holy hot springs of **Manikaran**, and the **Kheerganga** trek to a natural hot spring high in the mountains.

## How to reach Kasol

- **By air:** **Bhuntar** (Kullu–Manali) Airport is about 30 km away, around 1 hour by road.
- **By road:** overnight buses run from Delhi to **Bhuntar** (about 500 km), from where local buses and taxis go up the valley. Chandigarh is about 7 hours away.
- **By train:** the nearest major station is **Chandigarh**, or **Joginder Nagar** on the narrow-gauge line.

## Kasol

Kasol sits on the banks of the **Parvati river** at about 1,600 metres. Walk along the river, cross to the village of **Chalal** on the other bank through the pine forest, and spend time in the riverside cafés, which serve Israeli, Italian and Tibetan food as well as Himachali dishes.

## Manikaran Sahib

About 4 km from Kasol, **Manikaran** is a pilgrimage site for both Sikhs and Hindus.

- **Gurudwara Manikaran Sahib:** linked to Guru Nanak's visit, with free langar cooked partly in the natural **hot springs**.
- **Hot springs:** the water is hot enough to cook rice and lentils. Bathing pools are available, with separate areas for men and women.
- **Ram Temple** and **Shiva Temple:** old temples in the town.

## Villages of the Parvati Valley

- **Chalal:** a short forest walk from Kasol, with guesthouses and cafés.
- **Tosh:** a village at the end of the road, with views of snow peaks and a small waterfall.
- **Pulga and Kalga:** quiet villages near Barshaini, surrounded by forest and orchards. Pulga's "fairy forest" is a lovely walk.
- **Malana:** an isolated village with its own ancient system of village governance. Villagers ask visitors **not to touch** people, houses or temples, and to stay on the paths; respect these rules. The area is also known for illegal cannabis cultivation. **Drugs are illegal in India**, and police checks are common in the valley.

## The Kheerganga trek

**Kheerganga** is a meadow at about 2,950 metres, known for its **natural hot spring pool** with views of the mountains. According to legend, Kartikeya meditated here.

- **Start:** from **Barshaini**, about 15 km beyond Kasol by road.
- **Distance:** about **12 km** one way, through Nakthan village or via the Rudranag waterfall route.
- **Time:** 4 to 6 hours up.
- **Difficulty:** easy to moderate, with a steady climb and some rocky, slippery sections.
- **At the top:** bathe in the hot spring pool and enjoy the views. Food and basic shelter are available, but **rules on camping and overnight stays at Kheerganga have changed in recent years**, so check the current position before planning a night there.

**Tips:** start early, carry water, rain gear and warm layers, and don't trek after dark.

## Best time to visit

- **March to June:** pleasant weather, the best time for treks.
- **July to September:** monsoon rain, landslides and slippery trails.
- **October and November:** clear skies and autumn colours.
- **December to February:** cold, with snow in the upper valley; Kheerganga is snowbound.

## Safety tips

- Don't swim in the Parvati river; the current is very strong and the water freezing.
- Trek with company, and tell your guesthouse your plans.
- Respect local customs, especially in Malana.
- Stay away from drugs; possession is a serious offence.

## A 3-day plan

1. **Day 1:** arrive in Kasol, walk to Chalal, and visit Manikaran in the evening.
2. **Day 2:** Kheerganga trek from Barshaini (check current overnight rules, or return the same day).
3. **Day 3:** Tosh or Pulga and Kalga, then back to Kasol.

## Frequently asked questions

### How difficult is the Kheerganga trek?

Easy to moderate. Most reasonably fit people manage the 12 km in 4 to 6 hours.

### Can I stay overnight at Kheerganga?

Rules on camping and overnight stays have changed in recent years. Check the current position before you go.

### How far is Manikaran from Kasol?

About 4 km, a short drive or walk along the valley.
