**Chopta**, a meadow village at about 2,700 metres in Uttarakhand's Rudraprayag district, is often called the "Mini Switzerland of India". From here, a short but steep trail climbs to **Tungnath**, the **highest Shiva temple in the world**, and on to the summit of **Chandrashila**, which has one of the finest Himalayan panoramas you can reach on an easy trek. It is one of the best short treks in India for beginners.

## How to reach Chopta

- **By road:** Chopta is about 200 km from Rishikesh, around 8 to 9 hours via Rudraprayag and **Ukhimath**. Many travellers break the journey at Rudraprayag.
- **By train:** **Rishikesh** and **Haridwar** are the nearest major stations.
- **By air:** **Jolly Grant Airport**, Dehradun, is the nearest.

## Tungnath Temple

**Tungnath**, at about 3,680 metres, is one of the **Panch Kedar**, the five Shiva temples linked to the Pandavas of the Mahabharata. According to legend, Shiva's arms appeared here. The stone temple is believed to be more than a thousand years old.

- **The trek:** about **3.5 km** from Chopta on a paved trail, with a steady, steep climb. Most people take 2 to 3 hours up.
- **Opening season:** the temple is usually open from around **April or May to October or November**. In winter, the deity is moved to **Makkumath**, and the temple is snowbound.

## Chandrashila Summit

About **1.5 km** beyond Tungnath, a steeper trail climbs to **Chandrashila** at about **4,000 metres**. From the top, on a clear day, you see a 360-degree view of the Himalaya: **Nanda Devi, Trishul, Kedarnath, Chaukhamba** and **Bandarpunch**. Legend says Rama meditated here after defeating Ravana.

**Tip:** go up for sunrise if you are fit and prepared, with a headlamp, warm layers and a guide. Clouds usually build up by late morning.

## Deoria Tal

Near the village of **Sari**, about 25 km from Chopta, **Deoria Tal** is a lake at about 2,400 metres that reflects the Chaukhamba peaks on still mornings. It is reached by an easy 2.5 km walk from Sari. Many trekkers combine Deoria Tal with Chopta, Tungnath and Chandrashila in a 3 to 4-day circuit.

## Winter trek

In **winter (December to March)**, Chopta and the trail to Tungnath and Chandrashila are covered in snow, making this one of India's most popular **winter snow treks**. The temple is closed, but the trek and summit views are spectacular.

- Go with an experienced guide or a registered operator.
- Carry proper winter gear, gaiters and microspikes if needed.
- Road access to Chopta may be blocked after heavy snowfall; check conditions.

## A 4-day itinerary

1. **Rishikesh to Sari** (via Rudraprayag and Ukhimath).
2. **Deoria Tal** trek in the morning; drive to Chopta.
3. **Tungnath and Chandrashila** (sunrise or early morning), back to Chopta.
4. **Chopta to Rishikesh.**

## Difficulty and fitness

The trek is rated **easy to moderate**. Distances are short, but the climb is steep and at high altitude. Prepare with a few weeks of walking and stair climbing, and take it slowly.

## Altitude and safety

- **Acclimatise:** spend a night at Chopta or Sari before climbing to Chandrashila.
- **Watch for altitude sickness:** headache, nausea and breathlessness; descend if symptoms worsen.
- **Weather changes quickly:** start early and turn back if clouds or storms move in.
- **Carry cash**; ATMs are limited, and mobile networks are patchy.

## Best time to visit

- **April to June:** rhododendrons in bloom and pleasant weather.
- **September to November:** the clearest mountain views after the monsoon.
- **December to March:** snow treks.
- **July and August:** monsoon rain and landslides on the roads.

## Where to stay

Chopta has simple camps, guesthouses and a few lodges. Sari and Ukhimath have more homestays. Facilities are basic; carry warm clothes and a torch.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is the Tungnath trek?

About 3.5 km from Chopta to Tungnath, and a further 1.5 km to Chandrashila.

### Can beginners do the Chandrashila trek?

Yes. It is one of the best beginner treks in India, though the climb is steep and at high altitude.

### Is Tungnath open in winter?

No. The temple is closed in winter and the deity is moved to Makkumath, but the snow trek to Chandrashila is popular.
