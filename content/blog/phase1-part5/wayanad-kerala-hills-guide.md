**Wayanad**, a green hill district in north Kerala on the edge of the Western Ghats, is a land of misty mountains, coffee, tea and spice plantations, waterfalls and forests full of wildlife. It also has some of the oldest human traces in South India, in the carvings of the **Edakkal Caves**. It is a relaxed, nature-focused alternative to Munnar, and easy to combine with Coorg or Mysore.

**Before you go:** parts of Wayanad were hit by severe landslides in July 2024. Tourism in most of the district has continued, but some areas may be restricted. Check current local advisories, especially during the monsoon.

## How to reach Wayanad

- **By air:** **Kozhikode (Calicut)** Airport is about 90 km from Kalpetta, roughly 2.5 to 3 hours by road. Kannur and Bengaluru are other options.
- **By train:** **Kozhikode** is the nearest major railway station.
- **By road:** Kalpetta is about 280 km from Bengaluru via Mysore, and about 120 km from Mysore.

The main towns are **Kalpetta**, **Sulthan Bathery** and **Mananthavady**.

## Things to do in Wayanad

### Edakkal Caves

On **Ambukuthi Hill**, the **Edakkal Caves** are natural rock shelters with carvings believed to date back thousands of years, to the Neolithic period, including human figures, animals and symbols. Reaching them involves a steep walk and climb. Visitor numbers are limited per day, and the caves may close on certain days, so check timings before you go and arrive early.

### Chembra Peak and the heart-shaped lake

**Chembra Peak**, at about 2,100 metres, is the highest peak in Wayanad. The trek passes tea estates and grasslands to the famous **heart-shaped lake** partway up. Access is controlled by the forest department, with a limited number of trekkers each day and a guide required. Rules change, and the trek is sometimes closed, so check the current status first.

### Banasura Sagar Dam

**Banasura Sagar** is often described as the **largest earth dam in India**, with islands in its reservoir and the Banasura hills behind. Take a **speedboat or pedal boat** ride, or trek to viewpoints in the hills.

### Pookode Lake

A natural freshwater lake surrounded by forest, with pedal boats and a walking path. It's a pleasant stop on the way into Wayanad from Kozhikode via the **Thamarassery ghat road** with its hairpin bends.

### Waterfalls

- **Soochipara (Sentinel Rock) Falls:** a three-tiered waterfall reached by a forest walk.
- **Meenmutty Falls:** a tall waterfall reached by a trek through the forest.
- **Kanthanpara Falls:** a smaller, easy waterfall.

Waterfalls are strongest after the monsoon; don't swim in them in heavy rain.

### Muthanga and Tholpetty wildlife sanctuaries

Part of the **Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve**, these forests are home to **elephants**, gaur, deer, and sometimes tigers and leopards. Forest department **jeep safaris** run at set times, with limited seats. Check timings and closures.

### Kuruva Island

A group of small river islands on the Kabini, with dense forest and bamboo rafts. Visitor numbers are limited and the islands are sometimes closed, so check before visiting.

### Thirunelli Temple

An ancient Vishnu temple in the forest near the Karnataka border, known as the "Kashi of the South", where pilgrims perform rites for ancestors.

## Plantations and stays

Wayanad is known for **coffee, tea, pepper and cardamom**. Stay in a **plantation homestay** or **treehouse**, take a plantation walk, and learn how coffee and spices are grown. Buy spices, coffee and honey directly from farms.

## What to eat

Try **Malabar food**: **Kerala parotta and beef or chicken curry**, **appam with stew**, **bamboo rice payasam**, and the tribal-inspired dishes offered at some homestays. Wayanad coffee is excellent.

## Best time to visit

- **October to May:** the best time for sightseeing, treks and safaris.
- **June to September:** heavy monsoon rain, lush and beautiful, but with landslide risk; check advisories.

## A 3-day plan

1. **Day 1:** drive up the Thamarassery ghat, Pookode Lake, Banasura Sagar Dam at sunset.
2. **Day 2:** Edakkal Caves in the morning, Soochipara Falls, and a plantation walk.
3. **Day 3:** Chembra Peak trek (if open) or a Muthanga safari, then Kuruva Island or Thirunelli.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is it safe to visit Wayanad now?

Most of the district is open to visitors. Check current advisories, especially in the monsoon, and follow local guidance.

### Do I need a permit for Chembra Peak?

Yes. Access is controlled by the forest department, with daily limits and a guide required. Check whether the trek is open before you go.

### How many days are enough for Wayanad?

Three days covers the main sights at an easy pace.
