The Char Dham Yatra takes pilgrims to four sacred shrines high in the Garhwal Himalayas: **Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath**. Completing the circuit is one of the most important pilgrimages in Hinduism. It is also a serious mountain journey, with long drives, treks and changeable weather, so good planning makes all the difference.

## The four dhams

- **Yamunotri (about 3,290 m):** the source of the Yamuna, dedicated to Goddess Yamuna. A trek of about 6 km from Janki Chatti. Pilgrims cook rice in the hot spring of Surya Kund as prasad.
- **Gangotri (about 3,100 m):** dedicated to Goddess Ganga, reached by road. The actual source of the Ganga, Gaumukh, is a separate permit-controlled trek of about 18 km from Gangotri.
- **Kedarnath (about 3,583 m):** one of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Shiva, reached by a trek of about 16 to 18 km from Gaurikund, or by helicopter. See our [Kedarnath yatra guide](/blog/kedarnath-yatra-guide).
- **Badrinath (about 3,300 m):** dedicated to Vishnu as Badri Narayan, reached by road, on the banks of the Alaknanda. Take a dip in the **Tapt Kund** hot spring before darshan.

## The traditional order

The yatra is traditionally done from west to east: **Yamunotri → Gangotri → Kedarnath → Badrinath**. Most pilgrims start from **Haridwar, Rishikesh or Dehradun**.

## A 10 to 12-day itinerary from Haridwar

1. **Haridwar to Barkot:** a long drive via Mussoorie. Evening Ganga Aarti in Haridwar before you leave; see our [Haridwar guide](/blog/haridwar-ganga-aarti-guide).
2. **Yamunotri:** drive to Janki Chatti, trek up for darshan and return to Barkot.
3. **Barkot to Uttarkashi:** visit the Kashi Vishwanath Temple in Uttarkashi.
4. **Gangotri:** day trip for darshan and back to Uttarkashi, or stay at Harsil.
5. **Uttarkashi to Guptkashi or Sitapur:** a long mountain drive.
6. **Kedarnath trek:** from Gaurikund; stay the night at Kedarnath.
7. **Darshan and trek down;** return to Guptkashi.
8. **Guptkashi to Badrinath** via Chopta or Rudraprayag and Joshimath.
9. **Badrinath darshan, Tapt Kund and Mana village,** one of the last villages before the Tibet border, with Vyas Gufa and Bhim Pul.
10. **Badrinath to Rudraprayag or Srinagar (Garhwal).**
11. **Return to Rishikesh or Haridwar.**

Add one or two buffer days for weather, road closures or helicopter delays.

## Do Dham and helicopter yatras

- **Do Dham:** pilgrims short on time often visit just Kedarnath and Badrinath, in about 5 to 6 days.
- **Helicopter Char Dham packages:** operators fly from Dehradun to all four shrines in a few days. Book only licensed operators, and helicopter tickets for Kedarnath only through the official IRCTC Heli Yatra site.

## Registration and permits

- **Registration is mandatory** for all pilgrims on the official Uttarakhand tourism portal or app. Carry the slip and your photo ID.
- **Gaumukh trek:** needs a separate permit from the forest department, with a daily limit.
- Carry copies of IDs and vehicle papers; there are checkpoints along the route.

## Best time for the Char Dham Yatra

The shrines open around **Akshaya Tritiya (April or May)** and close around **Diwali and Bhai Dooj (October or November)**; exact dates are announced every year.

- **May and June:** peak season and the longest queues.
- **July to mid-September:** the monsoon brings landslides and closures. Avoid it if you can.
- **Mid-September to October:** clear skies, cooler weather and fewer people.

## Safety and health

- **Acclimatise** and don't rush; the dhams are all above 3,000 m.
- **Watch for altitude sickness:** headache, nausea and breathlessness. Descend if symptoms worsen.
- **Get a medical check-up** before the yatra if you are older or have heart, lung or blood-pressure problems.
- **Start drives early** and avoid driving after dark on mountain roads.
- **Follow weather alerts** from the Uttarakhand authorities, and don't cross landslide zones in heavy rain.

## What to pack

- Warm layers, a waterproof jacket, gloves and a woollen cap.
- Good walking shoes and a walking stick.
- Personal medicines, ORS, a small first-aid kit and a torch.
- Cash, since ATMs and phone networks are patchy in the hills.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many days does the Char Dham Yatra take?

About 10 to 12 days from Haridwar by road. A helicopter yatra can be done in 4 to 6 days.

### What is the correct order for the Char Dham Yatra?

Traditionally Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and then Badrinath, moving from west to east.

### Is Char Dham suitable for senior citizens?

Yes, with planning. Use helicopters, ponies or palkis for the treks, keep extra rest days and consult a doctor before you go.
