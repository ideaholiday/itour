Bir Billing, in Himachal Pradesh's Kangra valley, is India's best-known paragliding site and one of the best in the world. Pilots launch from **Billing**, a meadow at about 2,400 metres, and glide down over forests, tea gardens and villages to land at **Bir**, about 1,000 metres lower, with the Dhauladhar range behind them. The site hosted the **Paragliding World Cup in 2015**. For first-timers, a **tandem flight** with a qualified pilot is an unforgettable experience.

## How to reach Bir

- **By air:** Gaggal (Kangra) Airport is about 70 km away, around 2 hours by road.
- **By train:** Pathankot is the nearest broad-gauge station. The scenic narrow-gauge Kangra Valley Railway stops at **Ahju**, close to Bir, though services are slow and limited.
- **By road:** overnight Volvo buses run from Delhi to Bir or to nearby Baijnath. Delhi is about 500 km away.

Palampur (about 35 km) and Dharamshala (about 70 km) make good add-ons.

## How a tandem flight works

1. **Book through a registered operator** in Bir; they arrange transport up to Billing.
2. **Drive to Billing** (about 14 km of winding road), where the pilot sets up the glider.
3. **Briefing:** the pilot straps you into a harness in front of them and explains the take-off: you run a few steps down the slope until the wing lifts you.
4. **The flight:** usually **15 to 30 minutes**, depending on the package and conditions. Longer "cross-country" style tandem flights are sometimes offered on good days.
5. **Landing** at the landing site in Bir, usually gently on your feet or seated.

Many operators offer a GoPro video of your flight for an extra charge.

## The best time to fly

- **October and November:** the best season, with stable weather and clear views. This is when competitions and events happen.
- **March to May:** another good season, with warmer days.
- **December to February:** flights happen on clear days, but it is cold at take-off.
- **Monsoon (July to mid-September):** flying is suspended.

Flights always depend on the weather. Pilots may delay or cancel if the wind or clouds aren't right, so keep a spare morning.

## Choosing a safe pilot and operator

- Fly only with **operators and pilots registered with the Himachal Pradesh tourism department**.
- Ask about the pilot's **experience and licence**, and whether they are certified for tandem flights.
- Check the equipment: a reserve parachute, a helmet and a well-maintained harness.
- Avoid unusually cheap offers and touts; safety should decide, not price.
- Make sure the operator has **insurance** and a clear policy for weather cancellations.

## Who can fly?

- Most operators take passengers from about **12 years** old (with parental consent) up to healthy older adults.
- There are **weight limits**, usually up to about 90 to 100 kg, for safety.
- Tell the pilot if you have heart problems, back or neck issues, epilepsy or are pregnant; tandem flying may not be suitable.
- Wear closed shoes with ankle support, warm layers and sunglasses.

## Learn to fly

Bir has paragliding schools offering **beginner and intermediate courses** over one to two weeks, leading to solo flights. Choose schools with certified instructors and a strong safety record.

## What else to do in Bir

- **Tibetan colony:** Bir has a large Tibetan settlement with monasteries such as **Chokling Monastery** and the **Sherab Ling Monastery** nearby, and the **Deer Park Institute**, a centre for Buddhist and Indian philosophy.
- **Tea gardens:** walk through the tea estates around Bir, and visit tea factories in nearby Palampur.
- **Cafés:** Bir is known for relaxed cafés serving Tibetan, Israeli and Continental food.
- **Treks:** hike from Billing to **Rajgundha** and **Bara Bhangal**, or do shorter walks through pine forests.
- **Baijnath Temple:** a 13th-century Shiva temple about 15 km away.
- **Mountain biking** and camping at Billing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is paragliding in Bir Billing safe?

Tandem paragliding is generally safe when you fly with a registered, experienced pilot and proper equipment, and when flights are cancelled in bad weather.

### How long is a tandem flight in Bir Billing?

Usually 15 to 30 minutes, depending on the package and the conditions.

### What is the best month for paragliding in Bir?

October and November are the best, followed by March to May.

### Do I need any training?

No. For a tandem flight, the pilot does all the flying. You just need to follow the take-off and landing instructions.
