Meghalaya, "the abode of clouds", is one of India's most beautiful states: rolling pine-clad hills, deep gorges, some of the world's wettest places, waterfalls in every direction, and living bridges grown from tree roots by the Khasi people. Shillong, the capital, is the natural base for exploring it.

## How to reach Shillong

- **By air:** Guwahati's Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport is the main gateway, about 100 km away, roughly 3 hours by road. Shillong's own small airport at Umroi has limited flights.
- **By train:** Guwahati is the nearest major railway station.
- **By road:** shared taxis and cabs run from Guwahati to Shillong all day.

## Shillong

Shillong sits at about 1,500 metres and has a cool climate, a strong music scene and a lively market culture.

- **Umiam Lake (Barapani):** a large reservoir on the way from Guwahati, great for a stop and boating.
- **Ward's Lake:** a pretty lake garden in the city centre.
- **Don Bosco Museum:** a seven-storey museum of the cultures of Northeast India, with a rooftop skywalk.
- **Shillong Peak and Elephant Falls:** views over the city and a three-tier waterfall.
- **Police Bazaar and Bara Bazaar:** shopping, street food and local life.
- **Laitlum Canyons:** a dramatic viewpoint over deep valleys, about an hour away.

## Cherrapunji (Sohra)

About 55 km from Shillong, Cherrapunji is famous as one of the wettest places on earth, with nearby Mawsynram.

- **Nohkalikai Falls:** often described as the tallest plunge waterfall in India, falling around 340 metres into a green pool.
- **Seven Sisters Falls (Nohsngithiang):** seven streams falling side by side, at their best in the monsoon.
- **Mawsmai Cave:** a short, lit limestone cave you can walk through.
- **Arwah Cave** and **Dainthlen Falls**.

### The Double Decker Living Root Bridge

The most famous living root bridge in Meghalaya is at **Nongriat**, where two bridges grown from the roots of rubber fig trees stand one above the other. They were trained by Khasi villagers over generations.

The trek starts at **Tyrna** village and involves around **3,000 steep steps down** (and back up), crossing suspension bridges over river gorges. Allow a full day, or stay overnight at a homestay in Nongriat. Continue to the **Rainbow Falls** if you have energy.

**Tips:** start early, carry water and snacks, wear shoes with grip, and hire a local guide.

## Mawlynnong and Riwai

About 80 km from Shillong, **Mawlynnong** is known as one of the cleanest villages in Asia, with bamboo dustbins, flower gardens and a sky view platform built of bamboo. Nearby **Riwai** has an easily reached single-decker living root bridge, ideal for families who can't do the Nongriat trek.

## Dawki and the Umngot River

About 95 km from Shillong, on the Bangladesh border, the **Umngot river** is famous for water so clear in winter that boats seem to float on air. Take a boat ride from Dawki or **Shnongpdeng**, where you can also snorkel, kayak and camp by the river.

The water is clearest from **November to April**; in the monsoon it turns muddy.

## Best time to visit Meghalaya

- **October to April:** clear views, pleasant weather and the clearest water at Dawki. The best overall season.
- **Monsoon (June to September):** waterfalls at their most spectacular, but heavy rain, slippery treks and landslides. Plan flexible days.

## What to eat

Try Khasi food: **jadoh** (rice cooked with pork), **dohneiiong** (pork with black sesame), **tungrymbai** (fermented soybean), and **pumaloi** (steamed rice cakes). Shillong's cafés and street stalls also serve great momos and Tibetan food.

## A 5-day Meghalaya plan

1. **Guwahati to Shillong,** stopping at Umiam Lake; evening in Police Bazaar.
2. **Shillong sights:** Don Bosco Museum, Ward's Lake, Shillong Peak, Laitlum Canyons.
3. **Cherrapunji:** Nohkalikai, Seven Sisters and Mawsmai Cave; stay in Sohra.
4. **Double Decker Root Bridge trek** from Tyrna.
5. **Mawlynnong, Riwai and Dawki,** then back to Shillong or on to Guwahati.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many days are enough for Meghalaya?

Five days covers Shillong, Cherrapunji, the root bridge trek, Mawlynnong and Dawki. Add days for Mawsynram, Jowai or the Garo Hills.

### Is the Double Decker Root Bridge trek difficult?

It is a steep descent and climb of around 3,000 steps. Most reasonably fit people manage it in a day with breaks.

### When is Dawki's river clearest?

From about November to April, before the monsoon rains.
