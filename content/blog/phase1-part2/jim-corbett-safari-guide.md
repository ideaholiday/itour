Jim Corbett National Park in Uttarakhand is India's oldest national park, established in **1936** as Hailey National Park and later renamed after the hunter-turned-conservationist Jim Corbett. In **1973** it became the first reserve under **Project Tiger**. Today it is one of the best places in India to see Bengal tigers in the wild, along with wild elephants, leopards, deer, gharials and hundreds of bird species. It is only about 5 to 6 hours from Delhi.

## How to reach Corbett

- **By road:** Ramnagar, the gateway town, is about 250 km from Delhi via Moradabad.
- **By train:** **Ramnagar station** has direct trains from Delhi, including an overnight train.
- **By air:** Pantnagar Airport is about 80 km away; Delhi has far more flights.

Most resorts are around Ramnagar and along the Kosi river on the Dhikuli road.

## The safari zones

Corbett is divided into several safari zones, each with its own gate and landscape. The main ones are:

- **Bijrani:** open grasslands and sal forest close to Ramnagar; good for tigers and very popular.
- **Jhirna:** in the south of the park, drier forest; it usually **stays open all year**.
- **Dhela:** a newer zone next to Jhirna, also usually **open all year**, with good tiger sightings.
- **Dhikala:** the heart of the park, with vast grasslands (chaurs), the **Ramganga reservoir**, elephants in large herds and some of the best tiger sightings. Visited by **canter** (shared bus) or by staying overnight at the forest lodge.
- **Durga Devi:** hilly, forested and good for birds, including in the north-east of the park.
- **Garjia:** a smaller zone near the Garjia temple.

Zone opening seasons vary; core zones such as Dhikala and Bijrani usually open in the autumn and close for the monsoon. Check the current season dates on the official site.

## How to book a safari

- Safaris are booked on the **official Corbett Tiger Reserve online booking website**. Jeep permits are limited per zone and per slot, and sell out quickly on weekends and holidays.
- You need **ID details for every passenger** when booking, and must carry the same IDs at the gate.
- **Jeep safaris** take up to six guests with a registered driver and a forest guide. **Canter safaris** into Dhikala are shared buses.
- There are **morning and afternoon slots**. Morning safaris are usually better for sightings and cooler.

Beware of websites that look official but charge heavy "convenience" fees. Resorts and licensed operators can book for you, but make sure you receive the official permit.

## Staying inside the park at Dhikala

The **Dhikala Forest Rest House** complex lets you sleep inside the reserve, with morning and evening canter or jeep safaris from the lodge. Rooms and dormitories are limited and are booked on the official site, often months in advance. Facilities are simple, but waking up to the calls of deer and elephants is special.

## Best time for a Corbett safari

- **November to February:** cool, pleasant weather and good birding. Mornings are foggy and very cold in open jeeps.
- **March to June:** the best tiger-sighting months, as the forest thins and animals come to water. Hot in the afternoons.
- **July to mid-October:** most core zones close for the monsoon. Jhirna and Dhela usually stay open, weather permitting.

## Tips for a good safari

- Wear **earth-toned clothes** (green, brown, khaki) and avoid bright colours.
- **Dress in layers**; winter mornings are freezing in an open jeep.
- Carry **binoculars**, a hat, sunscreen and water.
- **Keep quiet** and follow your guide's instructions. Don't get out of the jeep.
- **Don't feed animals** or litter.
- Book **more than one safari**, ideally in different zones. Tiger sightings are never guaranteed, but more drives mean better chances.

## Other things to do around Corbett

- **Corbett Museum at Kaladhungi:** Jim Corbett's former home, with his belongings and letters.
- **Garjia Devi Temple:** a temple on a rock in the Kosi river.
- **Corbett Falls:** a small waterfall in the forest near Kaladhungi.
- **Birdwatching walks** along the Kosi river.
- **River angling** for mahseer with licensed operators, where permitted.

Nainital is about 2 hours away if you want to add a hill station.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is the best zone in Jim Corbett for tiger sighting?

Dhikala and Bijrani are the most famous for tigers. Jhirna and Dhela are good options, especially when the core zones are closed.

### How do I book a Jim Corbett safari?

On the official Corbett Tiger Reserve online booking website, with ID details for every guest. Book early for weekends and holidays.

### Is Jim Corbett open in the monsoon?

Most core zones close in the monsoon. Jhirna and Dhela usually remain open, weather permitting.

### How many safaris should I do?

At least two or three, ideally in different zones, to improve your chances of seeing a tiger.
