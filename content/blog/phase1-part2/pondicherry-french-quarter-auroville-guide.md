Pondicherry (officially Puducherry) is a small coastal town in South India with a very distinct character: yellow and white French colonial villas, bougainvillea-draped streets, a seaside promenade, a spiritual ashram and the experimental township of Auroville nearby. It was a French territory until 1954, and French street names, bakeries and cuisine still shape its charm.

## How to reach Pondicherry

- **By road:** Chennai is about 150 km away. The drive along the **East Coast Road (ECR)** takes about 3 to 4 hours and passes Mahabalipuram; see our [Mahabalipuram guide](/blog/mahabalipuram-temples-guide).
- **By train:** Puducherry station has direct trains from Chennai and some other cities. Villupuram Junction, about 40 km away, has more connections.
- **By air:** Chennai airport is the most practical option. Puducherry's small airport has limited flights.

## The French Quarter (White Town)

The neat grid of streets between the canal and the sea is best explored on foot or by bicycle. Walk along **Rue Romain Rolland, Rue Suffren and Rue Dumas**, admiring the colonial villas, the heritage hotels and the pastel walls.

**Sights in and around the French Quarter:**

- **Promenade Beach (Rock Beach):** a 1.5 km seafront walk that closes to traffic in the evenings and early mornings. Best at sunrise.
- **Sri Aurobindo Ashram:** founded in 1926, with the flower-covered samadhi of Sri Aurobindo and The Mother in a quiet courtyard. Silence is expected inside.
- **Notre Dame des Anges** and the neo-Gothic **Basilica of the Sacred Heart of Jesus**, known for its stained glass.
- **Manakula Vinayagar Temple:** a Ganesha temple with an elephant who blesses visitors.
- **Pondicherry Museum** and **French Institute**.
- **Bharathi Park** and the old lighthouse.

Cross the canal into the **Tamil Quarter** to see traditional Tamil houses with verandahs (thinnai), and the busy **Goubert Market**.

## Auroville

About 10 to 12 km north of the town, Auroville is an international township founded in 1968 by The Mother, where people from dozens of countries live and work together.

- Start at the **Visitors' Centre**, watch the short introductory film and walk to the **Matrimandir viewing point**. The golden sphere of the Matrimandir is at the heart of Auroville.
- **Entry to the inner chamber** of the Matrimandir is for concentration, not sightseeing, and needs advance booking in person at the Visitors' Centre. Rules can change, so check before you go.
- Visit Auroville's cafés, boutiques, and organic farms.

## Beaches around Pondicherry

- **Paradise Beach:** a sandbar reached by boat from the **Chunnambar Boat House**. Clean and relaxed.
- **Serenity Beach:** north of town, popular for **surfing lessons**. Surf schools rent boards and teach beginners.
- **Auroville Beach:** a lively local beach.

The sea around Pondicherry can be rough; swim only where it is safe and lifeguards are present.

## Things to do

- **Rent a bicycle or scooter** to explore the French Quarter and ride to Auroville.
- **Take a surf lesson** at Serenity Beach.
- **Scuba dive** with local dive centres (Pondicherry is one of the few places on India's east coast for diving).
- **Join a heritage walk** of the French and Tamil quarters.
- **Kayak in the backwaters** near Chunnambar.
- **Shop for handmade paper, pottery, incense and fabrics** from Auroville and ashram units.

## What to eat

- **French bakeries** for croissants, baguettes and pastries.
- **Creole (Franco-Tamil) cuisine**, such as fish curries with French touches.
- **Wood-fired pizzas and crêpes** in the White Town cafés.
- **South Indian breakfasts**: idli, dosa, pongal and filter coffee in the Tamil Quarter.
- **Fresh seafood** along the beach roads.

## Best time to visit

October to March is ideal. The northeast monsoon brings heavy rain from October to December, and cyclones sometimes hit the coast, so check forecasts. Summers (April to June) are hot and humid. Surfing is good for much of the year, with bigger waves during the monsoon months.

## A 3-day plan

1. **Day 1:** Promenade at sunrise, the French Quarter, Sri Aurobindo Ashram, basilica and cafés.
2. **Day 2:** Auroville and the Matrimandir viewing point, then Serenity Beach.
3. **Day 3:** Paradise Beach by boat, the Tamil Quarter and Goubert Market.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many days are enough for Pondicherry?

Two to three days for the town, Auroville and the beaches.

### Can I go inside the Matrimandir?

Only with advance booking at the Auroville Visitors' Centre, and only for concentration, not sightseeing. Everyone can see it from the viewing point.

### Is Pondicherry good for a weekend from Chennai?

Yes. It is about 3 to 4 hours by road along the East Coast Road.
