## PRO TIPS FOR MAXIMUM CONVERSIONS

### 1. **Timing (Most Important)**
- **Best time:** 9-10 AM or 6-7 PM (when checking WhatsApp)
- **Avoid:** 1-3 PM (siesta), 11 PM+ (sleeping)
- **For broadcasts:** 9 AM Monday-Thursday (highest engagement)

### 2. **Message Spacing**
- For 2-message: Wait 20-30 seconds between sends (looks deliberate, not spammy)
- For 3-message: 15s → 30s delays (natural conversation feel)

### 3. **Emojis (Don't Overdo)**
- Keep to 1-2 per section
- Use: 🎉🚀💰📱 (action-oriented)
- Avoid: Too many 😂❤️ (feels unprofessional)

### 4. **Links**
- **Desktop users copy easier:** Put link at end OR in standalone line
- **Mobile users prefer:** Simple, no UTM parameters
- Test: `supply.ideaholiday.in` works better than full URLs

### 5. **Follow-Up Sequence**
```
Day 1: Template A or B (initial)
Day 3: Template G (gentle follow-up)
Day 5: Template G (stronger follow-up)
Day 7 (48h before): Template H (urgency)
```

### 6. **Personalization (Optional But Powerful)**
Replace generic greeting with:
- "Hi [Name]," instead of generic
- "Fellow tour operator" (builds tribe feeling)
- "From one travel pro to another" (peer angle)
