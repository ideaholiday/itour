# 7. Profile, enquiries, share kit and plans

> **Summary:** The supplier's public profile page, traveler enquiries, the share kit (QR codes, printouts, reviews widget), the Verified badge and Spotlight plans, and the subscription new suppliers need.
> **Read when:** a supplier wants more visibility or reviews, asks about the Verified badge or pricing, or sees "Subscription needed".

[Back to the guide](README.md)

## 7.1 Public profile

**Public profile** tab. Every supplier has a free public page at `ideaholiday.in/suppliers/<profile-link>` that travelers find on Idea Holiday and on Google.

**Status boxes at the top:**
| Box | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| Visible to travelers | Yes, unless the supplier hid it or Idea Holiday suspended it |
| On Google | Only **after KYB approval**. Paying is not needed. |
| Badge | Verified or Not verified (§7.4) |
| Profile completeness | How much of the profile is filled in |

Also: **Copy link** and **Share on WhatsApp** (ready message: "See our reviews and ask us anything on Idea Holiday: <link>").

**Edit profile:**
| Field | Notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| Profile link | The `/suppliers/...` address. If changed, old links keep working. Some words are reserved. |
| Tagline | Up to 120 characters, e.g. "Airport transfers and day tours across Goa" |
| About your business | Up to 2000 characters: years operating, what you run, vehicles or guides, what travelers love |
| Logo | Square image, under 5 MB |
| Cover photo | Wide photo of vehicles, team or tours. Also used when the link is shared. Under 5 MB. |
| Languages, Cities you operate in | Comma separated |
| Your other pages | Website and social links. **Not shown to travelers.** Only Google uses them. |

Buttons: **Save profile**, **Show my profile / Hide my profile**.

**What a profile never shows:** email, phone, contact name, GSTIN, PAN, bank details, Supplier ID.

**Contact details are blocked in the text.** Phone numbers, emails, links and WhatsApp numbers are refused. Travelers contact the supplier through **Enquiries** so bookings stay on Idea Holiday.

**Products on the profile:** listings appear on the profile **only with a paid Spotlight** (§7.4). They are always bookable through normal marketplace search.

**Review stars on Google** appear only with **3 or more** reviews from real bookings.

> **Tell the supplier:** "Add a real cover photo and a clear 'About'. Share your profile link on WhatsApp, Instagram and your visiting card. It's free, and after KYB approval it can show on Google."

## 7.2 Enquiries

**Enquiries** tab: questions travelers sent from the supplier's public profile.
- Only signed-in travelers can send enquiries.
- One conversation per traveler per supplier. A new question continues it.
- The supplier sees the traveler's **first name only**.
- **Messages with phone numbers, emails or links are refused** and not sent. Contact details are shared only with a booking.
- Enquiries needing a reply are marked **Needs reply**. A conversation can be closed.

> **Tell the supplier:** "Reply within the hour if you can. A fast, helpful answer is the best way to win the booking. Don't try to take the traveler off the platform; those messages are blocked."

## 7.3 Share kit

**Share kit** tab: tools that bring people to the profile or review page, with visit counts.

| Piece | What for |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Profile QR** | For vehicles, office, social media. Opens the public profile. |
| **Review QR** | For after the trip. The traveler confirms their booking reference and phone, then leaves a **verified** review. |
| Downloads | QR as **SVG** or **PNG** (1024 px) |
| **A5 standee** / **3-inch sticker** | Print-ready pages. Open → Print → Save as PDF. The Verified badge appears only if earned. |
| **Reviews widget** | Code to paste into the supplier's own website. Shows their rating and 3 latest reviews. |
| **Voucher QR** | Added automatically to every paid booking's voucher: "After your trip, scan to review". |
| **Visits from your share kit** | Opens per channel (QR, standee, sticker, voucher, widget, link): last 30 days and all time |

- Nothing about the person scanning is stored.
- If the profile is **hidden**, the codes don't open anything until it's shown again.
- Opening the share kit creates a review link automatically if the supplier has none.

> **Tell the supplier:** "Stick the review QR inside every vehicle. Drivers can ask happy travelers to scan it at drop-off. Only real bookings can review, so it's safe."

## 7.4 Verified badge and Spotlight

Bought in the **Plans** tab → **Profile plans**. Any **KYB-approved** supplier can buy them.

| Plan | Price (+18% GST) | What it gives |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Verified** | ₹999 / year | A yearly business check. The badge is shown **only if the check passes**. |
| **Spotlight** | ₹2,999 one-time | Shows one published listing on the public profile. No renewal. |
| **Verified Plus** | ₹3,499 first year | Verified check + one Spotlight. Renews as Verified (₹999). |

**The Verified badge is never sold. Payment buys the check.**
- Checks: business identity (GSTIN or PAN), bank account **in the business name**, business address, and a call with the owner. Tourism registration and vehicle permits are recorded where relevant.
- **Passed:** badge for 365 days. Renewing early adds a year.
- **Rejected:** Verified is refunded in full. Verified Plus refunds only the check part (about ₹590 of ₹4,128.82 at launch prices); the Spotlight is kept.
- Only one check can be pending at a time. Status shows as "Your Verified check is with our team (since …)".
- Reminders 30, 7 and 1 days before the badge ends.
- The badge is removed at once if KYB is suspended, and an admin can revoke it with a reason.

**Spotlight rules:**
- Choose a **published** listing. One listing can be in one Spotlight at a time.
- **Move a Spotlight** to another listing **once a year** ("You can move it again in a year").
- If the listing stops being bookable, it drops off the profile but the Spotlight isn't lost.

How to buy: choose the plan (and listing, for a Spotlight) → optional coupon → **See price** → **Pay** (Cashfree). If the payment completed but the screen didn't update, press **I've paid, check**. Invoices are under **Payments and invoices**.

> **Known gap:** the Public profile tab still says "Contact support to start" for the Verified badge. Suppliers can now buy the check themselves in **Plans**.

> **Team note:** never say "pay and get Verified". Say "pay for the Verified **check**; the badge appears if you pass, and you get a refund if you don't."

## 7.5 Subscription

**Who needs one:** suppliers who **signed up on or after 14 September 2026**. Older suppliers are exempt for good.

**Without cover:** listings stay visible but **can't take new bookings**. Existing bookings, trips and payouts continue normally. The Overview shows "Subscription needed — not receiving new bookings".

**What counts as covered:**
| Cover | Plans tab shows |
| :--- | :--- |
| Free **launch offer** (every new supplier while it runs) | "Free launch offer, until <date>" |
| **Waiver** by Idea Holiday (admin, with a reason) | "Subscription waived by Idea Holiday…" |
| **Paid** subscription | "Subscribed…" |
| **100% coupon** | Activated at once, no payment |

**Price:** set by admins. As of 15 Sep 2026 the price isn't set, so the Plans tab says "The subscription isn't on sale yet. You're covered by the launch offer until it is." When it goes on sale: price + 18% GST, supplier coupons allowed, Cashfree payment, a GST tax invoice (numbered like `IHS/2026-27/00001`).
- Renewing early loses nothing; the new period starts when the current paid cover ends.
- Reminders 30, 7 and 1 days before a dated cover ends.
- There is no auto-renewal. Each renewal is a new payment.

> **Tell the supplier:** "Right now you're on the free launch offer. We'll remind you before it ends, and your existing bookings are never affected."
