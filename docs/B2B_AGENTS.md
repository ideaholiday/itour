# B2B Travel Agents: Idea Holiday

> **Summary:** Rules for IdeaHoliday's own travel agents: who may apply, how an agency is approved, what an agent pays (with GST on our fee), and what the agent's client sees in the agency's brand.
> **Read when:** changing agency sign-up or review, agent prices, agent bookings or their notifications. Decisions: [`DECISIONS_B2B.md`](DECISIONS_B2B.md). Plan: [`plans/b2b-travel-agents.md`](plans/b2b-travel-agents.md). Not a supplier's own agents ([`SUPPLIER_OPERATIONS.md`](SUPPLIER_OPERATIONS.md)).

## Sign-up and review
1. **A traveler account applies** at `/agents/signup` with a GSTIN or a PAN (a GSTIN's PAN is filled in; if both are given they must match). Supplier, staff and admin logins can't apply.
2. **An admin reviews** at `/admin/agencies`: approve with a 5–10% discount (5% until set), reject a pending application with a reason the agency sees, or suspend an approved one. Approving an approved agency changes its discount. Every decision is audited; the agency is emailed when its status changes.
3. A rejected agency fixes its details and applies again (back to pending). An approved or suspended one can't reapply.

## Agent price
1. **Only an `APPROVED` agency** gets it. It is the website price less the agency's `discount_pct`, rounded to the rupee, priced by the server on the quote, the hold and the booking.
2. **Out of commission, its own budget.** It is not part of the 10% giveaway cap ([`BUSINESS_RULES.md`](BUSINESS_RULES.md) §3.2) and always stays below the booking's commission: IdeaHoliday keeps at least ₹1. The supplier's payout never moves.
2a. **18% GST on IdeaHoliday's service fee, added on top (ADR 055).** The service fee is the booking's commission less the agent discount; the agent pays the agent price plus 18% of that fee, rounded to the rupee. Both are frozen on the booking (`agent_service_fee_inr`, `agent_service_gst_inr`): `amount_inr + agent_discount_inr (+ wallet credit) = commission_amount + supplier_payout_amount + agent_service_gst_inr`. A circuit's GST is worked per booking on its own commission when the order is made.
3. **No coupon, friend discount or creator attribution** on an agent booking (`400 AGENT_NO_COUPONS` for a code). Wallet credit may pay part. Payment is prepaid (Cashfree or wallet).
4. **Every listing type and circuits.** The booking's source is `IH_B2B`. A circuit quote freezes each line's agent discount; an order from it is refused (`409 AGENCY_NOT_APPROVED`) once the agency isn't approved, and its total is net of the discount.
5. A suspended agency is back on website prices at once; bookings already made are unchanged.

## Who owns and hears what
1. **The agency owns the booking**: it is on the agency's account, cancellations and wallet refunds go to the agency. The guest is the agent's client, named on the voucher.
2. **Money goes to the agency, the trip to the guest.** The agency gets the confirmation with the invoice, document resends, cancellation, refund, reschedule, amendment and "confirmation pending" messages. The guest gets a confirmation with the voucher only, and driver, reminder and pickup messages. No review invite goes to the guest, who has no account.
3. **White-label for the client (ADR 055).** The voucher is headed by the agency's logo (or name), phone and email, with "Your travel agent" and no IdeaHoliday name, review QR, referral link or price; the pickup-code notice says the agent shares the code. Guest emails come in the agency's name (our verified address) with replies to the agency; their subject starts with the agency's name and their text drops any line naming IdeaHoliday, My Trips or our helpline and ends "For any change, contact <agency> (<phone>). — <agency>". WhatsApp comes from our number: with `WHATSAPP_TEMPLATE_AGENT_GUEST_UPDATE` set the client gets that neutral template, otherwise the existing ones, which name IdeaHoliday. Links stay on ideaholiday.in.
3a. **The agency's logo** is a PNG, JPG or WEBP the agent uploads on `/agents` (`POST /api/uploads`, then `PUT /api/agents/logo`); only an image the same account uploaded is accepted (`400 INVALID_LOGO`).
3b. Voucher and invoice links of an agent booking last until 7 days after the trip (at least 30 days). From the dashboard, "Send to client" opens WhatsApp to the guest's number with the voucher link, and "Copy voucher message" copies the same text.
3c. **The pickup code** is not on the client's voucher. The dashboard shows it on each upcoming, unused booking (the agency owns the booking, so it sees what a traveler sees in My Bookings), with Copy, and "Send to client" includes it with the warning to give it only after checking the driver and vehicle. It is never in the CSV statement.
4. **The agent dashboard** (`/agents/dashboard`) lists the agency's bookings by trip or booking date with paid, saved and refunded amounts, opens the client's voucher and the agency's invoice, cancels single bookings (circuit legs from the circuit's page), and downloads the statement as CSV. A suspended agency keeps its history.
5. **The tax invoice is IdeaHoliday's, billed to the agency** with its GSTIN and address: the website price, the agent discount, the agent price and IdeaHoliday's service fee within it, then GST @ 18% on the service fee, and the total paid. The service fee carries SAC 998555 at 18% (owner's CA, 2026-10-01; 998552 is the CA's broader alternative; `BUSINESS_AGENT_SAC` overrides it).
