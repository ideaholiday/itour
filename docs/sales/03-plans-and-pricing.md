# 3. Plans and Pricing

> **Summary:** What Idea Holiday charges suppliers: commission, the subscription (free during the launch offer; price not set yet), and the paid profile plans Verified, Spotlight and Verified Plus. Also covers which plan to offer to which supplier, and coupons.
> **Read when:** a supplier asks "what does it cost?", or you are selling a plan.

## 3.1 Everything at a glance

| What | Price | Who pays | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Joining | Free | — | No setup fee |
| **Commission** | **30%** of each Idea Holiday booking (default) | Deducted from the payout | Only on marketplace and partner bookings. Fixed on each booking when it is made. |
| Commission on the supplier's own sales | **0%** | — | Walk-in, phone, manual, agent and reseller bookings |
| **Subscription** | **Not set yet.** Free under the launch offer | Suppliers who joined on or after 14 Sep 2026 | Needed to take new bookings. See 3.2. |
| **Verified** check | **₹999 + GST** a year (₹1,178.82) | Optional | Refunded if the check fails |
| **Spotlight** | **₹2,999 + GST**, one time (₹3,538.82) | Optional | Shows one listing on the public profile. No renewal needed. |
| **Verified Plus** | **₹3,499 + GST** (₹4,128.82) | Optional | The first-year check plus one Spotlight. Renews as Verified. |

All plan prices are + 18% GST. The supplier gets a GST tax invoice for every payment.

## 3.2 The subscription

**What to say today:**
> "There's a subscription for new partners, but right now it's **free** under our launch offer. You don't pay anything to start."

Rules you must know:

1. Suppliers who joined **before 14 Sep 2026** don't need a subscription. It is free for them for life.
2. Suppliers who join **from 14 Sep 2026** need one to take **new** bookings. While the launch offer is on, every new supplier gets it free automatically when they sign up.
3. **The launch offer has no end date yet.** When the owner sets one, every launch supplier's free period ends on that date. The supplier gets a reminder by email and WhatsApp 30, 7 and 1 days before.
4. **The price is not decided yet.** Until an admin sets it, the subscription can't be bought, and suppliers stay on the free launch offer.
5. If cover ends, **bookings already made are always honoured**, and the profile stays visible. Only new bookings stop.
6. An admin can give one supplier a free waiver for any length of time, with a reason. Ask your manager. Use it for strategic suppliers only.

> **Never** quote a subscription price or say "free forever" to a supplier who joined after 14 Sep 2026. Say "free during the launch offer; we'll tell you well in advance before that changes."

When the price is set, this section will be updated with the price, the billing period and the pitch.

## 3.3 Verified check

- **What they buy:** a yearly business check, **not** the badge. The check covers business identity (GSTIN or PAN), a bank account in the business name, the business address and a call with the owner. Tourism registration and vehicle permits are recorded when they apply.
- **If it passes:** the **Verified** badge shows on their profile for 365 days.
- **If it fails:** the full payment is refunded.
- **Who can buy:** only suppliers whose KYB is approved. Only one check can be pending at a time.
- **Renewal:** reminders go out 30, 7 and 1 days before the badge ends. Renewing early adds the year to the end date, so they lose nothing.

**Pitch:** *"Travelers compare 5 operators and book the one they trust. The Verified badge tells them we checked your business ourselves. And if you don't pass, you get your money back, so there's no risk."*

## 3.4 Spotlight

- Shows **one** published listing on the supplier's public profile. This is the only way listings appear on the profile page (marketplace search is not affected).
- One-time payment, no renewal.
- The Spotlight can be moved to another listing **once a year**.
- If the listing stops being bookable, it drops off the profile but the Spotlight isn't lost.

**Pitch:** *"When someone opens your page from Google or your QR standee, they see your best tour right there and can book it."*

## 3.5 Verified Plus (the best value)

Verified check plus one Spotlight for **₹3,499 + GST**, instead of ₹3,998 + GST bought separately. It renews as plain Verified.

If the check fails, only the check part is refunded (₹590 at today's prices). **The Spotlight is kept.** Tell the supplier this before they pay.

## 3.6 Which plan to offer

| Supplier | Offer |
| :--- | :--- |
| Just signed up, KYB not done | Nothing yet. Focus on getting them live. |
| Live, fewer than 3 reviews | Free share kit and reviews first. Offer Verified after 2 to 4 weeks. |
| Live, getting bookings, has a strong main product | **Verified Plus** |
| Many listings, uses QR standees and Google a lot | **Verified Plus**, or Verified + Spotlight |
| Already Verified, renewal due | Renew Verified. Offer a Spotlight if they have none. |
| Wants only more visibility, not the check | **Spotlight** |

## 3.7 Coupons for suppliers

- Admins create supplier coupons in Admin → Coupons. There are two kinds: **"For: supplier plans"** (Verified, Spotlight, Verified Plus) and **"For: supplier subscriptions"**. Traveler coupons don't work on plans.
- The discount comes off before GST.
- A **100% coupon** activates the plan at once with no payment. For a 100% Verified coupon, a failed check gives no refund, since nothing was paid.
- Ask your manager for a coupon code. Don't invent one. Use them to close a deal or for founding partners.

## 3.8 How the supplier pays

In the portal: **Plans** tab → pick a plan → apply a coupon if they have one → pay through **Cashfree** (UPI, card, net banking). The plan activates only after the payment is confirmed. The GST invoice (number like `IHS/2026-27/00001`) is available right after.

Payments are **not** automatic renewals. The supplier pays again each time.

## 3.9 Commission: what to know

- **30% by default.** Admins can set a different rate for a supplier or a single product (0–50%), with a reason. You cannot promise a lower rate. Ask your manager, who asks an admin.
- The rate is **fixed on each booking** when it is made. A change applies to new bookings only, and the supplier gets a notice.
- Coupons, referral discounts and creator commissions all come out of the 30%, never out of the supplier's payout. Together they can't exceed 10% of the booking value.
