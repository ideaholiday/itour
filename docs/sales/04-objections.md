# 4. Objection Handling

> **Summary:** Honest answers to the pushback suppliers give most often, such as commission, trust, "no time", "not tech-savvy" and payouts. Every answer is true to how the product works today.
> **Read when:** a supplier says no, or before a call with a hesitant supplier.

[Back to the sales kit](README.md)

**Method:** listen fully → agree with the concern → answer with a fact → ask a question to move forward. Never argue, and never make up a feature.

---

**"30% commission is too much."**
> "I understand. Three points. First, you pay it only when we send you a paying customer, someone you'd never have got otherwise. Second, any discount or coupon comes out of our 30%, not yours. Third, on your own walk-in, phone and agent customers you pay **0%**, and you still use our software for free. Think of it as your marketing cost: you pay only when it works. Which of your products has empty seats most days? Let's start with that one."

*Don't offer a lower rate yourself. For a large or strategic supplier, escalate to your manager.*

---

**"I already get enough customers."**
> "That's great, then the free tools will help you most: one seat count for walk-ins, phone and agents, so you're never overbooked; staff logins; a guest list with what's still to collect. And for your slow days and off-season, online bookings fill the empty seats."

---

**"What does the subscription cost? What's the catch?"**
> "Right now new partners get it **free** under our launch offer. The price for later isn't decided yet, and we'll tell you well in advance, with reminders at 30, 7 and 1 day. Even if cover ends, every booking you already have is honoured."

*Never quote a price. Never say "free forever" to someone who joined after 14 Sep 2026.*

---

**"I'm not good with technology."**
> "Most of our partners run on WhatsApp and a notebook, and this is built for them. I'll set up your first listing with you on the phone right now. After that you mostly just get a WhatsApp message when someone books."

For drivers without a smartphone: *"You can assign the driver and confirm by phone. The driver doesn't need the app."*

---

**"I don't have time."**
> "Sign-up takes 2 minutes and verification can take seconds if you have your GSTIN handy. Give me 10 minutes now and your first listing can be live today."

---

**"When do I get paid? Is my money safe?"**
> "The traveler pays online before the trip. After the trip, your share goes into a settlement to your bank account, and the portal shows every payout's status and the bank reference."

*Don't promise a fixed number of days. Say the cycle Finance actually runs.*

---

**"What if the traveler cancels at the last minute or doesn't show up?"**
> "Less than 24 hours before, or a no-show, and the traveler gets no refund and you're paid in full. Between your free-cancellation deadline and 24 hours, you get 50%. Before your deadline, they get a full refund. You set the deadline yourself: 24, 48 or 72 hours."

*Tell them: if a traveler doesn't show up, don't cancel the booking. Cancelling refunds them in full. Report the no-show to operations.*

---

**"Can I put my phone number on my profile?"**
> "Not on the page. That keeps bookings safe on Idea Holiday, for you and the traveler. Travelers can message you through Enquiries, and you get their details once they book."

---

**"Can I just buy the Verified badge?"**
> "No, and that's exactly why travelers trust it. You pay ₹999 + GST for the business check. If you pass, you get the badge for a year. If you don't, you get your money back."

---

**"I already use Bókun / FareHarbor."**
> "We connect to systems that support the OCTo standard, so your products and seats can come across. Let me check your setup with our tech team and come back to you."

*Don't promise a one-click connection. See [file 6](06-do-not-promise.md).*

---

**"Other portals took my money and sent nothing."**
> "Joining is free and the subscription is free during launch. You pay commission only on real, prepaid bookings. There's no risk to trying it."

---

**"I don't have GST."**
> "That's fine for listing. You'll need your PAN for payouts. Without GSTIN, our team reviews your documents (PAN card and transport licence) by hand instead of in seconds."

*Known gap: an attraction or activity business with no GSTIN and no transport licence can't be approved today. Check with the owner before promising a date.*
