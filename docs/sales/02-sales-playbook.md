> **Read when:** planning your day's calls or getting ready for a supplier meeting.

## 2.1 Who to target

Idea Holiday is built **first for offline suppliers**: businesses that run on phone calls, WhatsApp and notebooks. They gain the most from us.

| Priority | Segment | Examples | Best hook |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A** | Taxi and transfer operators with 2 or more cars | Airport pickups, city-to-city cabs, outstation trips | Online bookings paid in advance, automatic driver assignment, pickup OTP |
| **A** | Day tour operators | City tours, sightseeing (private or shared, SIC) | Live seats and instant confirmation; walk-ins and online sales from one seat count |
| **A** | Activity businesses | Water sports, scuba, paragliding, cooking classes, workshops | Counter sales and online sales without overbooking; QR check-in |
| **B** | Package operators and DMCs | Golden Triangle, Goa 3N/4D, Lucknow – Ayodhya – Varanasi | Agents with credit limits and net rates; package quotations are coming soon |
| **B** | Attractions | Parks, museums, shows | Ticket tiers (adult, child, senior, free infant), QR entry |
| **C** | Suppliers already on Bókun, FareHarbor and similar systems | Larger, tech-ready operators | Connect through OCTo. **Check [file 6](06-do-not-promise.md) first.** |

**Don't target:** self-drive rentals, peer-to-peer drivers with no business, hotels on their own (we don't sell rooms alone), flight agents. These are outside what Idea Holiday sells.

## 2.2 Where to find them

- Google Maps: "taxi service", "tour operator", "water sports" in each target city.
- JustDial and IndiaMART listings.
- Airports, railway stations and tourist spots: the counters and the cars parked outside.
- Tourism department registration lists.
- Referrals: after every onboarded supplier, ask: *"Who else in your area should be on Idea Holiday?"*
- Supplier WhatsApp groups and local operator associations.

## 2.3 The 60-second pitch

> "Namaste, I'm ___ from Idea Holiday. We're an online travel marketplace, like GetYourGuide for India. We bring travelers who book and **pay online before the trip**.
>
> Joining is **free**, and right now new partners get the subscription **free** as a launch offer. You only pay a commission when we send you a booking. On your own customers you pay **nothing**.
>
> You also get free software: your walk-in and phone bookings, agents, drivers and guest list in one place, so you're never overbooked.
>
> It takes 10 minutes to sign up. Can I help you set it up now?"

## 2.4 Discovery questions

Ask these before pitching features. Then pitch the 2 or 3 benefits that match their answers.

| Ask | If they say… | Pitch this |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| How do customers book with you today? | Phone, WhatsApp, walk-in | Online bookings and instant confirmation |
| How many cars, seats or slots do you run a day? | More than they can fill | New customers from the marketplace and Google |
| Have you ever double-booked a seat or a car? | Yes | One live seat count across all channels |
| Do you work with travel agents? How do you track what they owe? | Notebook or Excel | Agents with credit limits and statements |
| Do customers ever cancel late or not show up? | Yes, and we lose money | They pay online in advance; no refund if they cancel less than 24 hours before |
| How do you assign drivers today? | Phone calls | Driver roster, automatic assignment, pickup OTP |
| How do customers find you online? | They don't, or only through Google Maps | Public profile, reviews, share kit QR codes |
| Do you have GSTIN and business PAN? | Yes | "Then you can be approved in seconds." |

## 2.5 Call flow

1. **Open** (15 seconds): who you are, why you're calling.
2. **Discover** (2 minutes): 3 or 4 questions from 2.4.
3. **Pitch** (1 minute): only the benefits that match their answers.
4. **Close:** "Shall we sign you up now? It takes 10 minutes."
5. **Sign up on the call** if you can: go through [file 5](05-onboarding-checklist.md) together.
6. **If not now:** send WhatsApp message 1 (below) and book a follow-up date.
7. **Log it:** name, city, segment, phone, outcome and next date.

## 2.6 WhatsApp messages

### Message 1: first contact / after a call

**English**
> Hi {Name}, this is {You} from Idea Holiday.
> We help {tour / taxi / activity} businesses get online bookings, paid in advance.
> ✅ Free to join, and the subscription is free under our launch offer
> ✅ Commission only on bookings we send you
> ✅ 0% on your own walk-in and phone customers
> ✅ Free software for walk-ins, agents, drivers and guest lists
> Sign up in 10 minutes: https://supply.ideaholiday.in
> Can I call you to help set it up?

**Hinglish**
> Namaste {Name} ji, main {You}, Idea Holiday se.
> Hum {tour / taxi / activity} walon ko online booking dilate hain, payment advance mein.
> ✅ Joining free, launch offer mein subscription bhi free
> ✅ Commission sirf humari bheji booking par
> ✅ Aapke apne walk-in aur phone customers par 0%
> ✅ Walk-in, agent, driver, guest list sab ek jagah, free
> 10 minute mein sign up: https://supply.ideaholiday.in
> Kab call karun setup ke liye?

### Message 2: signed up, KYB not done

> Hi {Name}, welcome to Idea Holiday! One step left: open the **Compliance** tab, enter your GSTIN and business PAN, and press **Verify All**. If they match, you're approved in seconds.
> Please don't send PAN or bank details on WhatsApp. Enter them only in the portal.

### Message 3: approved, no listing yet

> Congratulations {Name}, you're approved! Now add your first listing: go to Listings → New listing. Add clear photos and your real price. Then open **Seats & schedule** and save it, so bookings confirm instantly. I can do it with you on a call. What time suits you?

### Message 4: live, time to grow

> Hi {Name}, your listing is live 🎉 Three free ways to get more bookings:
> 1. Download your QR standee and stickers from **Share kit** and put them in your office and cars.
> 2. Ask every happy customer for a review. Better reviews rank you higher.
> 3. Share your profile link on your WhatsApp status.

### Message 5: sell Verified or Spotlight (only after they are live)

> Hi {Name}, travelers trust businesses with the **Verified** badge. For ₹999 + GST a year we check your business (GST or PAN, bank account, address and a call with you). If you pass, the badge shows on your profile for a year. If you don't pass, you get your money back.
> Want your best tour shown on your profile too? **Verified Plus** (₹3,499 + GST) includes a Spotlight. Buy it in the **Plans** tab.
