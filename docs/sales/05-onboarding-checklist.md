# 5. Onboarding Checklist: From "Yes" to First Booking

> **Summary:** The steps to take a supplier from agreeing to join through sign-up, KYB, first listing and first booking, with what you do at each step, what usually goes wrong, and team targets.
> **Read when:** a supplier has said yes, or you are checking why a signed-up supplier isn't getting bookings.

[Back to the sales kit](README.md)

## 5.1 Before the call, ask the supplier to have ready

- [ ] Business name (the real trading name) and city
- [ ] A work email and mobile number (for WhatsApp alerts)
- [ ] **GSTIN** and **business PAN** (the PAN inside the GSTIN, not a partner's personal PAN)
- [ ] Bank account in the business name
- [ ] If there's no GSTIN: PAN card and transport licence, as PDF or photo (max 5 MB each)
- [ ] 3 to 5 good photos of each product, as image links
- [ ] Price list, schedule (days and start times) and seats or vehicles per trip
- [ ] Cancellation policy (e.g. free cancellation up to 24, 48 or 72 hours before)

## 5.2 The steps

| # | Step | Where | Done when | Common problem |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Sign up** | supply.ideaholiday.in → Create partner account | They get a Supplier ID (`sup_…`) | They signed up as a traveler. Use the supplier sign-up. |
| 2 | **KYB** | Compliance tab → enter GSTIN + PAN → **Verify All** | Status is **APPROVED** | PAN doesn't match the GSTIN; they typed a personal PAN. |
| 3 | **Bank account** | Compliance → payout bank account | Bank verified | Account not in the business name |
| 4 | **First listing** | Listings → New listing | Listing created | Photos must be links; there's no upload yet. |
| 5 | **Check the listing before publishing** | Wizard review step | Title, description and photos are right | **Title, description and photos can't be edited later.** Check them carefully. |
| 6 | **Publish** | Listings panel → Publish Live | Listing is PUBLISHED | — |
| 7 | **Seats and schedule** | Listing → Seats & schedule → Save | Days, times, seats and prices saved | Without this, every booking needs manual acceptance within **10 minutes**. |
| 8 | **Drivers** (transport only) | Fleet | At least one driver with email, seat count and vehicle category | No driver means no automatic assignment. |
| 9 | **Test it** | Open ideaholiday.in and search their city | The listing shows with live dates | See 5.3 |
| 10 | **Share kit** | Share kit panel | QR standee and stickers downloaded | — |
| 11 | **First booking** | — | 🎉 | Follow up in [file 2](02-sales-playbook.md#27-follow-up-rhythm) |
| 12 | **Growth call, 30 days later** | — | Plan offered | See [file 3](03-plans-and-pricing.md#36-which-plan-to-offer) |

> **Transfers:** a transfer listing has no Seats & schedule button, so transfer bookings always need manual acceptance. Tell the supplier to keep WhatsApp alerts on and accept within 10 minutes. A booking not accepted in time moves to another supplier or to operations.

## 5.3 "Nobody can see or book my listing": check in this order

1. **KYB APPROVED?** This is the most common cause.
2. **Listing PUBLISHED?**
3. **Subscription covered?** (Only for suppliers who joined on or after 14 Sep 2026.) See the Plans tab. Under the launch offer it should be automatic. If it's missing, escalate to an admin.
4. **The date is open?** Not sold out, blocked, or past the cut-off.

## 5.4 Team targets (suggested; your manager sets the real ones)

| Metric | Per person, per week |
| :--- | :--- |
| New suppliers contacted | 50 |
| Sign-ups | 10 |
| KYB approved | 7 |
| Live (listing published + schedule saved) | 5 |
| Plans sold (to suppliers live 30+ days) | 2 |

Track each supplier by stage: **Contacted → Signed up → KYB approved → Live → First booking → Paid plan.** The biggest drop is usually between Signed up and KYB approved, so call them back within 48 hours.

## 5.5 Escalation

| Problem | Send to |
| :--- | :--- |
| KYB rejected, no GSTIN, suspension | Admin (supplier approval) |
| Lower commission request, subscription waiver | Your manager → admin |
| Coupon for a plan | Your manager |
| Portal not doing what this kit says | Tech team |

Always include the **Supplier ID** and a screenshot.
