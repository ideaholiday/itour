# 6. Never Promise These

> **Summary:** Things the sales team must not say or promise, because the product doesn't work that way or the owner hasn't decided yet. One broken promise loses a supplier and their referrals.
> **Read when:** before your first sales call, and whenever you aren't sure a feature works.

[Back to the sales kit](README.md)

The full technical list is in [Supplier guide §9.2 Known gaps](../supplier-guide/09-faq-and-known-gaps.md#92-known-gaps-dont-promise-these). This page covers what matters in a sales conversation.

## 6.1 Money and plans

| Don't say | Say instead |
| :--- | :--- |
| "The subscription costs ₹___" | "It's free during the launch offer. The later price isn't set yet." |
| "Free forever" (to a supplier who joined from 14 Sep 2026) | "Free during the launch offer. You'll get reminders well before that changes." |
| "I can give you 20% commission" | "The standard rate is 30%. I'll ask my manager about your case." |
| "You'll be paid every Monday" (or any fixed day) | Tell them the cycle Finance actually runs. There's no automatic schedule. |
| "Buy the Verified badge" | "Buy the Verified check. You get the badge if you pass, or a refund if you don't." |
| "The plan renews automatically" | "You renew it yourself. We remind you 30, 7 and 1 days before." |

## 6.2 Features

| Don't promise | The truth today |
| :--- | :--- |
| "You can edit your listing's title, photos or description any time" | They can't be edited after creation. Make a corrected copy and unpublish the old one. |
| "Upload your photos" | Photos are image links only. |
| "Choose Instant or Request approval in the wizard" | That setting isn't used. Bookings are instant only after **Seats & schedule** is saved. |
| "Transfers confirm instantly" | Transfer bookings always need acceptance within 10 minutes. |
| "You have 24 hours to accept a booking" | It's **10 minutes** by default. |
| "Connect Bókun or FareHarbor in one click and import everything" | Only a standard OCTo connection fully works. Check with tech first. |
| "Bulk publish, pause or change prices" | The bulk buttons don't work yet. |
| "Your dashboard shows today's and this month's earnings" | Those overview numbers and charts are not real yet. Use the Bookings and Settlement screens. |
| "Package quotations with PDFs and agent pricing" | Still being built. Don't demo it until tech confirms it's live. |
| "Cancel a no-show booking to keep the money" | Cancelling refunds the traveler 100% and pays the supplier nothing. Report no-shows to operations instead. |
| "Any business can be approved without GST" | Without GSTIN, approval needs a transport licence, even for activities. Check with the owner first. |

## 6.3 Never do

- Never ask for PAN, GSTIN, bank details or documents on WhatsApp, email or a phone call. They go only in the portal's Compliance tab.
- Never share one supplier's prices, bookings or commission with another supplier.
- Never create a supplier account with your own email, or log in as the supplier.
- Never promise a traveler-side feature, such as a discount or refund, to close a supplier deal.
- Never promise a date for something the tech team hasn't confirmed.
