# 1. What the Supplier Gains

> **Summary:** What a tour operator, transport company, attraction or activity business gets from Idea Holiday, in plain words. You can send this page, or parts of it, to suppliers.
> **Read when:** preparing a pitch, or answering a supplier who asks "what's in it for me?".

[Back to the sales kit](README.md)

## In one line

**Idea Holiday brings you new customers online, and gives you free software to run your whole business: online bookings, walk-ins, agents, drivers and payments, all in one place.**

## 1. More customers

| What you get | Why it matters |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Listed on ideaholiday.in** | Travelers search for and book your tours, transfers, packages, tickets and activities. |
| **Your own public page** at `ideaholiday.in/suppliers/<your-name>` | Google can show it once your business is verified (KYB). You don't pay to appear on Google. |
| **City pages** | Travelers browsing a city (for example "suppliers in Goa") see your business. |
| **Enquiries** | Travelers can message you through your page before they book. |
| **Reviews that count** | Only travelers who really booked can leave a rating that counts. You can't get fake bad reviews from people who never travelled. |
| **Higher ranking with good reviews** | Search ranks suppliers by verified review rating, so good service brings more bookings. |
| **Sell through partners** | Your listings can also be sold by Idea Holiday's API partners, if you switch that on. |

## 2. You get paid safely

| What you get | Why it matters |
| :--- | :--- |
| **The traveler pays online before the trip** | No cash collection and no "I'll pay you later" for online bookings. |
| **Your price is locked** | Once a traveler books, nobody can lower your price. |
| **Discounts come from our share, not yours** | Coupons and referral discounts reduce Idea Holiday's commission. Your payout stays the same. |
| **No-shows and late cancellations are paid** | A traveler who cancels less than 24 hours before, or doesn't turn up, gets no refund, and you get your full share. |
| **Clear payout breakdown** | Every booking shows the fare, the commission and your net payout. |

## 3. No overbooking, less phone work

| What you get | Why it matters |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Live seats** | Set your capacity once. Seats go down automatically as online, partner and counter bookings come in. You can't be overbooked. |
| **Instant confirmation** | Once you set your seats and schedule, bookings confirm automatically, with no phone calls. |
| **Your own prices and rules** | Seasonal rates, weekend or festival prices, promotions, minimum and maximum group size, and a booking cut-off time. |
| **Close dates easily** | Block a date, a vehicle or your whole business for a holiday or bad weather. |
| **QR vouchers** | Every traveler gets a voucher with a QR code. Scan it to check them in. |
| **WhatsApp and email alerts** | You hear about every new booking right away. |

## 4. Run your whole business in one place

These tools are **free** for your own customers. You pay **no commission** on sales you make yourself.

| What you get | Why it matters |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Walk-in, phone and manual bookings** | Sell at your counter from the same seats. Record cash, UPI, card or bank payments, part payments and what the customer still owes. |
| **Your agents** | Add your travel agents, give each one a commission and a credit limit, and see what each one owes you. |
| **Staff logins** | Your manager, front desk and guides each get their own login. They never see your bank details or KYB. |
| **Departures board and guest list** | See who is travelling on each trip, how much is still to collect, and check guests in by QR. |
| **Crew assignment** | Put guides, vehicles and equipment on each departure. The system stops you double-booking a guide or vehicle. |
| **Reschedule in one click** | Move a booking to another date. The seats move with it and the price stays the same. |
| **Cancel a whole departure** | For bad weather or too few people: everyone is refunded and informed automatically. |
| **Reseller keys** | Give your own resellers a key so they can book your inventory directly. |

## 5. For transport operators: drivers and safety

| What you get | Why it matters |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Driver roster** | Add your drivers and vehicles. The system can assign a driver to each trip automatically. |
| **Pickup OTP** | The trip starts only when the driver enters the traveler's code, so the right car picks up the right guest. |
| **Driver app** | Drivers see their trip and share live location with the traveler. |
| **Toll, permit and GST worked out for you** | Transfer prices include interstate permits, tolls and GST, so there are no surprises for the traveler. |

| What you get | Why it matters |

## Worked example (share with the supplier)

A traveler books a ₹10,000 day tour online:

| | Amount |
| :--- | :--- |
| Traveler pays | ₹10,000 |
| Idea Holiday commission (30%) | ₹3,000 |
| **Your payout** | **₹7,000** |

If the traveler used a ₹500 coupon, they pay ₹9,500, but **your payout is still ₹7,000**. The coupon came out of our ₹3,000.

The same day you sell 5 seats at your counter for ₹2,000 each: you keep **all ₹10,000**, and the seats come off the same inventory, so the online site never oversells.

> **Team note:** payout timing. There is no fixed automatic weekly or monthly cycle. Finance creates settlement batches. Tell the supplier the cycle Finance actually runs; never guess.
