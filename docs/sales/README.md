# Sales Kit: Onboard Suppliers and Sell Plans

> **Summary:** Sales kit for the Idea Holiday supplier team. It covers who to call, what to say, what a supplier gains, what our plans cost, how to handle "no", and how to take a supplier from first call to first booking.
> **Read when:** you are prospecting, pitching or onboarding a supplier, or selling a Verified check, a Spotlight or the subscription.

Checked against the product on 2026-09-29. Prices and rules come from [`SUPPLIER_PLANS.md`](../SUPPLIER_PLANS.md) and [`BUSINESS_RULES.md`](../BUSINESS_RULES.md). If this kit and the portal disagree, the portal is right. Tell the tech team so the kit can be fixed.

## Your goal

1. **Onboard as many good suppliers as possible.** "Good" means KYB approved, at least one published listing, and a first booking within 30 days.
2. **Sell paid plans** (Verified, Spotlight, Verified Plus) to suppliers who are live. When the subscription price is set, sell the subscription too.

## Three rules for every sales call

1. **Never promise what the product doesn't do.** Read [file 6](06-do-not-promise.md) before your first call.
2. **Never collect PAN, GSTIN, bank details or documents on WhatsApp or email.** The supplier enters them in the portal's **Compliance** tab.
3. **Never sell the Verified badge.** The supplier pays for a business check. The badge appears only if they pass, and if they fail they get a refund.
