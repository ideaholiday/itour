## PROFESSIONAL GUIDELINES

### **Tone & Voice**
- ✅ Trustworthy, not salesy
- ✅ Clear value proposition (not feature dumping)
- ✅ Professional but conversational
- ✅ Always close with tagline: "From Idea to Itinerary"

### **Formatting**
- ✅ Short lines (mobile-friendly)
- ✅ Bullet points for clarity
- ✅ Minimal emojis (2-3 per message max)
- ✅ Always include sender: "— Idea Holiday"

### **CTA Standards**
- Every message ends with: `supply.ideaholiday.in`
- Include tagline footer
- Optional: `"Questions? We're here to help"`

### **Emoji Use (Branded, Minimal)**
- 👋 Greeting
- 🎯 Focus/target
- 📊 Data/results
- 💰 Pricing
- ✅ Benefits
- ⏰ Urgency
- ❌ Avoid: 🎉🔥😂 (too casual)

### **Timing Recommendations**
| **Message Type** | **Best Day/Time** | **Spacing** |
|---|---|---|
| Initial (A/B/C) | Tue–Thu, 10 AM IST | — |
| Multi-message | Same timing | 20-30s between |
| Follow-up | 3 days after | — |
| Urgency | 48h before deadline | — |
